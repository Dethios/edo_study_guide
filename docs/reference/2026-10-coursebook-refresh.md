  the guide gives JFRP as the current answer.

  extractable PDF text. They support the RRAB, MEIA, JAR, and Service-autonomy discussion. The complete August manual
  and CJCSI 5123.01J were not independently retrieved; the guide identifies the reproduced excerpts precisely.
- The September organization slides mark the SYSCOM/PAE structure as changing. Coursebook labels are attributed to
  those editions; older public-source checks retain their original dates. This pass does not certify every incumbent,
  reporting relationship, statute, or policy page as current in October.
- The MDAP thresholds already matched the new coursebook: $1B RDT&E or $4.5B procurement in FY2024 constant dollars.
- The revised PPBE organization diagram (slide 25) was visually checked: Total Force was N1, PRIME was N5,
  Sustainment was N4, RCP was N8, and OWA combines elements of N2/3/5/6/9.
- Similarity below measures normalized extracted text, not visual equivalence or a TLO-completeness score. Pure image
  changes can escape that comparison. Leadership modules have module-level coverage mappings; they have not received
  a new objective-by-objective certification. Introduction/administration are retained as reference, not board teaching.
