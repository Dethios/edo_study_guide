## IWS 6.0 vs PMW 150

Board answer:

> IWS 6.0 is the PEO IWS combat-system C2 and sensor-netting lane; PMW 150 is
> the PEO C4I operational/tactical C2 application lane. IWS 6.0 helps the combat
> system generate, share, and use tactically useful tracks through products like
> CEC. PMW 150 gives commanders and watch teams C2 applications, COP/CTP,
> planning tools, and tactical data-link interfaces through products like GCCS-M,
> MTC2, C2P, and Link 16 modernization.

## CANES vs ADNS vs MIDS/Link 16

Board answer:

> CANES is the shipboard afloat network and compute environment. ADNS is the
> tactical WAN/router layer that moves shipboard IP traffic off the ship over
> available communications bearers. MIDS/Link 16 is the tactical data-link
> terminal/waveform/network used for tactical information exchange, not the same
> as the ship's general network infrastructure.

## CIVPERS Poor Performer Removal

Board answer:

> For non-probationary employees, the fastest lawful removal path is often a
> Chapter 75 adverse action for unacceptable performance under 5 U.S.C. 7513,
> coordinated with HRO and counsel. Chapter 75 does not require a PIP, but it
> requires due process, preponderance of evidence, a reasonable and accurate
> performance measurement, efficiency-of-service nexus, and Douglas Factor
> analysis. Chapter 43 remains available, but it requires a PIP/opportunity
> period before reduction in grade or removal.
