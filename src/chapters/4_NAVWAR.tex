\subsection{Project Overmatch}
\begin{description}[style=nextline,labelsep=0.5em,font=\bfseries]
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Deliver a resilient Naval Operational Architecture that links platforms, weapons, sensors, and data fabrics for Distributed Maritime Operations and \ac{cjadc2}; \ac{navwar} provides the digital architecture, data transport, and cyber \ac{ta} that underpin the hybrid fleet.
  \item[\textbf{Five Eyes coalition network.}] Under an Oct~2024 Project Arrangement with Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom, Cooperative Project Personnel embed in San Diego to co-develop kill-chain networks; Capt.\ Remil Capili (PM), \ac{vadm} Seiko Okano (steering chair/\ac{pmildep}), Shayna Bond (international deputy), and David Flowers (international lead) oversee the coalition lab and \ac{rimpac}~26 demonstrations\FlagBilletNote{\ac{vadm} Seiko Okano is the \ac{pmildep} flag billet holder in Appendix~\ref{app:edo-flags}}~\autocite{DVIDS-Overmatch-FVEY-2025-Nov}.
  \item[\textbf{Allied exercises.}] Overmatch \ac{fvey} solutions are being exercised at Trident Warrior 24, Talisman Sabre 25, and a capstone during \ac{rimpac}~26 to prove resilient comms in contested electromagnetic environments\autocite{DVIDS-Overmatch-FVEY-2025-Nov}.
  \item[\textbf{Rapid onboarding.}] Open \ac{dagir} and Maven Smart Systems pipelines let \ac{navwar} ingest commercial \ac{aiml} tools in weeks, accelerating fleet readiness visualizations and data-driven decision aids across the hybrid force~\autocite{DVIDS-Overmatch-OpenDAGIR-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Governance.}] As \ac{pmildep} and \ac{drpm} Overmatch, \ac{vadm} Okano keeps digital architecture tied to Program Decision Meetings and \ac{peo} portfolio trades, ensuring Overmatch data standards propagate into new ship, sub, and aircraft programs~\autocite{DVIDS-Overmatch-FVEY-2025-Nov,SECNAV5000-2G}.
\end{description}
