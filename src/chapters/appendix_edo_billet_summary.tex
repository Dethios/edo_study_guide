\begin{description}[style=nextline, labelsep=0.5em, font=\bfseries]
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Quick reference to where \acp{edo} have billets on the November~2025 main slate, grouped by community and sorted by location with billet counts by rank. Current as of 2025-11-01.
  \item[\textbf{Method.}] Parsed the ``SLATE\_EDO\_MAIN'' worksheet in \emph{EDO Main Slate November 2025.xlsx} by mentor group (community), location (AHPORT), and billet rank (BRANK); rank order follows flag to junior officer seniority (\acs{vadm}, \acs{radm}, \acs{rdml}, CAPT, CDR, LCDR, LT, LTJG).
  \item[\textbf{Location expansions.}] Location codes were expanded best-effort based on billet context (e.g., ANNAP~$\rightarrow$~Annapolis, MD; MONTEY~$\rightarrow$~Monterey, CA). Confirmations are appreciated for potentially ambiguous codes listed after the table.
\end{description}
