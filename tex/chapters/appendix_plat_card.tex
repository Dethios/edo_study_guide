\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Life-cycle cost: acquisition cost is only part of total ownership; sustainment dominates many board scenarios.
  \item Cost estimate: know when to use \ac{poe}, \ac{ice}, component cost estimate, \ac{lcc}, and Will Cost/Should Cost language from the detailed chapters.
  \item \ac{ppbe}: planning sets strategy, programming builds the \ac{pom}, budgeting defends the \ac{bes}, and execution obligates and monitors funds.
  \item Reprogramming: check purpose, time, amount, threshold, and congressional prior-approval requirements before moving money.
  \item Product improvements: choose \ac{rdte}, procurement, or O\&MN based on development/testing, end-item procurement, and maintenance/installation purpose.
  \item Appropriation life cycle: availability for obligation, expiration, and cancellation are separate gates.
\end{itemize}
