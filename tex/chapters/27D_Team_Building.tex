\section{Team Building}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{IPT focus.}] \ac{ippd} and \acp{ipt} are central to acquisition execution; empowered cross-functional teams improve integration and decision quality~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Team dynamics.}] Effective teams share clear goals, empowered members, balanced productivity and satisfaction, and constructive conflict management~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Leadership impact.}] \ac{ipt} leaders act as supervisors, facilitators, and coaches while managing stakeholder relationships and resources~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Cohesion stages.}] Teams progress through forming, storming, norming, performing, and adjourning; leaders must adapt at each stage~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{IPPD and IPT Foundations}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{IPPD definition.}] \ac{ippd} integrates essential acquisition activities with multidisciplinary teams to optimize design, manufacturing, and supportability across the life cycle~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{IPT definition.}] An \ac{ipt} is a multidisciplinary team collectively responsible for delivering a defined product or process, composed of empowered representatives across functions~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{IPT purpose.}] \acp{ipt} build successful programs, identify and resolve issues, and make sound, timely recommendations to enable decisions~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{IPT Types and Roles}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Oversight IPTs.}] \acp{oipt} assist the \ac{mdauthority} with investment decisions and ensure programs are structured and resourced to succeed~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Working-level IPTs.}] \acp{wipt} are formed by the program manager with \ac{osd} staff and stakeholders to focus on topics like cost/performance, program baselines, acquisition strategy, test and evaluation, and contracting~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Program IPTs.}] \acp{pipt} represent functional areas and organizations involved in designing, developing, testing, manufacturing, deploying, and supporting products or services~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Team Characteristics}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Effective teams.}] Clear purpose and goals, empowered participation, top management support with bottom-up leadership, effective communication, balanced productivity and satisfaction, cohesiveness, and objective process reviews~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Ineffective teams.}] Unclear goals, lack of management support, unempowered members, weak planning, limited training, poor access to lessons learned, unequal participation, inadequate resources, and unrealistic schedules~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{IPT Leadership Requirements}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Leadership roles.}] \ac{ipt} leaders may serve as supervisors, facilitators, and coaches while interfacing with upper management and serving on higher-level teams~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Core skills.}] Leaders must manage disputes, orchestrate communication, obtain resources, build stakeholder relationships, and apply group process skills with flexibility and empowerment~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Group Decision Making}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Consensus focus.}] Collaboration and consensus improve commitment and solution quality, but can be time-consuming and vulnerable to difficult personalities~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Decision commitment.}] The goal is a shared decision that members understand, discussed, and can support~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Virtual Teaming}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Inclusion.}] Actively include remote members, establish core hours, and use multiple communication channels to maintain cohesion~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Cohesion practices.}] Recognize remote contributions through awards, celebrations, and shared events to reinforce team identity~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Team Development Stages}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Forming.}] Members learn roles, expectations, tasks, and ground rules while deferring to authority and establishing mission and charter~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Storming.}] Members challenge ideas, form alliances, and raise personal issues; leaders must work through conflict or the team never gels~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Norming.}] Members build trust, establish boundaries, and express issues constructively; norms re-emerge after storming~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Performing.}] Teams collaborate with open communications, manage risk while innovating, and handle conflict constructively~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Adjourning.}] Members close out tasks, capture lessons learned, and transition focus to follow-on work~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Building Effective Teams}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Start-up actions.}] Make introductions, clarify purpose, define operating agreements, set roles and responsibilities, and open communication channels~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Direction.}] Draft mission and vision statements, produce a team charter, and identify critical success factors to sustain a positive atmosphere~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Leadership posture.}] Use participative leadership, manage conflict, and reinforce balanced participation~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \ac{ippd} relies on empowered, multidisciplinary \acp{ipt} to integrate life-cycle decisions~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item Effective \acp{ipt} combine clear goals, empowerment, communication, and balanced productivity~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item Teams progress through forming, storming, norming, performing, and adjourning~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
  \item Strong team charters and participative leadership enable sustained performance~\autocite{edo-5-1-5-team-building-2025}.
\end{itemize}
