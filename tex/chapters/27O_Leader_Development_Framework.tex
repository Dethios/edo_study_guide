\section{Leader Development Framework}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Navy framework.}] The \ac{nldf} supports \ac{nds} objectives and the Navy design to develop
  leaders who improve themselves and their teams faster than adversaries~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{EDO framework.}] The \ac{ldf} aligns \ac{edo} development with character, competence, and
  culture outcomes and embeds mentoring as a core community practice~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Development lanes.}] Education, on-the-job training, and self-guided learning build balanced
  leaders across technical, character, and team dimensions~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Mentoring and coaching.}] Career counseling focuses on milestones, mentoring builds
  long-term relationships, and coaching targets discrete development needs~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Command readiness.}] Command screening emphasizes mission focus, \ac{eq}, character,
  communication, and competence as predictors of command success~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Navy Leader Development Framework}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Strategic alignment.}] The \ac{nldf} supports the \ac{nds} and Navy design by maximizing the
  performance of Sailors through deliberate leader development~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Leader expectations.}] Effective leaders commit to continuous growth, uphold Navy core
  values, and build competence, character, and culture within their teams~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Leader Development Lanes}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Schools.}] Education and certification programs provide foundational knowledge and
  professional credibility~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{On-the-job training.}] Qualifications, \ac{edqp}, and command-sponsored development translate
  learning into operational competence~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Self-guided learning.}] Reading, mentoring, and professional development activities sustain
  growth and reinforce the \ac{grgb} mindset~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{EDO Leader Development Framework}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Community strategy.}] The \ac{ldf} provides a structured path to develop \ac{edo} leaders for
  major command and equivalent positions while aligning to CNO guidance~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Mentoring policy.}] Formal mentoring is embedded in the \ac{ldf} and supports character,
  competence, and culture development over the notional career path~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Mentoring, Coaching, and Career Counseling}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Career counseling.}] Focuses on milestones and timing for assignments and promotions~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Mentoring.}] Builds ongoing relationships that integrate professional and personal
  development beyond milestones~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Coaching.}] Uses time-bound engagements to target specific development needs or skills~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Command Screening Attributes}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Command readiness.}] Command screening evaluates mission focus, \ac{eq}, character,
  communication, and competence as predictors of success in command~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \ac{nldf} and \ac{ldf} align leader growth with Navy strategic priorities and community needs~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item Development lanes include schools, on-the-job training, and self-guided learning~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item Mentoring, coaching, and career counseling serve different development purposes~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item \ac{edqp} and mentoring are central to EDO leader development planning~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
  \item Command screening emphasizes mission, \ac{eq}, character, communication, and competence~\autocite{edo-5-2-10-leader-development-framework-2025}.
\end{itemize}
