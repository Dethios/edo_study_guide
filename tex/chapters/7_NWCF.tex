\section{Field Activity Financial Management}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{General Fund.}] Direct appropriations flow through the budget-execution chain and buy the activity's own
  mission work; use purpose, time, and amount controls before deciding whether the color of money is available.
  \item[\textbf{NWCF.}] \ac{nwcf} activities operate as buyer-seller, reimbursable providers: customer orders create work,
  stabilized rates recover full costs, and \ac{nor}/\ac{aor} drive future rate corrections rather than profit
  maximization~\autocite{USC-2208-NWCF,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch1,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
  \item[\textbf{Order choice.}] Use Economy Act work requests for routine services that must stay within the cited funds'
  availability; use project orders only for specific, tangible end products where the project-order rules support
  performance beyond expiration without using the order merely to park funds~\autocite{DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch2,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch3}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Cost categories}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Direct costs.}] Costs directly attributable to a project (for example, materials, labor, and travel tied to a specific job)~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Indirect costs.}] Costs that cannot be attributed to a single project (for example, security, executive salaries, and shared services)~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{description}

\note{Direct costs typically include labor, material, and \ac{odc}; indirect costs include overhead and \ac{ganda}~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.}

\subsection{Financial management systems}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mission funded (General Fund).}] Appropriated funds flow down the chain of command to execute mission needs; the goal is to budget adequate resources without excess~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Working capital funded (NWCF).}] A reimbursable system where activities are not directly appropriated for customer work; they recover costs from customers and aim to break even over time under working-capital-fund policy~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,USC-2208-NWCF,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch1}.
\end{description}

\note{General Fund activity management is a \textit{``checking account''} model; \ac{nwcf} is a \textit{``credit card''} model. Some \ac{nwcf} commands have mission-funded units, but purpose, time, and amount controls still apply and funds cannot be mixed casually~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,USC-2208-NWCF,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch1}.}

\subsection{General Fund operations}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Appropriation flow.}] \ac{asnfmc} (Echelon I) distributes budget authority to Echelon II \acp{bso}; \acp{bso} issue allotments and \acp{eob}. Echelon III field activities issue allowances and \acp{optar} to subordinate units at Echelon IV. See \Tab{\ref{tab:budget-execution-authority-chain}} for the formal appropriation, apportionment, allocation, and allotment chain~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,OMB-A11,DoDFMR-Vol3}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Appropriated versus reimbursable funds for mission-funded activities}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Appropriated funds.}] Pay for mission labor (civilian salaries), mission non-labor (materials, travel, contracts), and all indirect costs (overhead)~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Reimbursable funds.}] Used when a mission-funded activity performs work for a customer; reimbursements cover direct labor and other direct costs, but not indirect costs~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{General Fund budgeting cycle (EOB)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Mid-year review of the current year budget~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item Answer budget data calls~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item Submit next year's (future) budget~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item Marks and reclamas (summer review)~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item Sept/Oct: end of current FY, start of next FY; receive \ac{eob} and execute~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What is the NWCF?}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Definition.}] \ac{nwcf} is a reimbursable operation that provides goods or services to customers and recovers the full cost of output~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,USC-2208-NWCF,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch1}.
  \item[\textbf{Criteria to join.}] Output costs must be identifiable, customers must be known, and the advantages and disadvantages of the buyer-seller relationship must be evaluated before establishing or moving work into a working-capital-fund activity~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch2}.
\end{description}

\subsection{NWCF cycle of operations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Congress provides corpus to begin operations~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,USC-2208-NWCF,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch1}.
  \item The \ac{nwcf} activity receives a reimbursable work request via a funding document~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item The activity performs work; direct and indirect costs are financed by the corpus~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch12}.
  \item Costs are priced using stabilized rates held constant during execution~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
  \item The customer is billed for costs incurred; payment reimburses the corpus~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch11}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Stabilized labor rates (SLR)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] \ac{slr} reimburses \ac{nwcf} activities for work performed and is established during the budget process to recover full costs, prior period gains/losses, and approved capital surcharges~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch12}.
  \item[\textbf{Formula.}]
        \[
          \text{SLR} = \frac{\text{Direct labor} + \text{Indirect (overhead/G\&A)} + \text{AOR recoupment}}{\text{Direct hours}}
        \]
  \item[\textbf{Components.}] Direct labor, indirect expenses (including \ac{ganda}), and \ac{aor} recoupment to achieve break-even~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Operating results: NOR and AOR}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{NOR.}] Current-year profit or loss based on revenue minus expenses~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
  \item[\textbf{AOR.}] Cumulative gains and losses since inception; \ac{aor} is managed at Navy level and used to set rates~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
  \item[\textbf{Rate adjustment.}] Gains reduce rates two years out; losses increase rates two years out to drive \ac{aor} toward zero in the budget year~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
\end{description}

\subsection{NWCF budget timeline}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Feb--Apr.}] Internal budget formation at the activity level~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Apr--May.}] Activity budgets consolidate to activity group and submit to the \ac{bso}~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{May--Jun.}] \ac{bso} submits the activity group budget to \ac{fmb}~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Jul--Aug.}] \ac{fmb} submits the activity group budget to \ac{dod} for the \ac{bes}~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Fall review.}] The activity group budget is reviewed through the \ac{bes}/President's Budget build rather than being submitted directly to Congress in September~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,OMB-A11}.
  \item[\textbf{Jan--Feb.}] The President submits the President's Budget to Congress within the statutory January-to-February window; \ac{nwcf} rates are set 18--24 months in advance through the budget process~\autocite{USC-1105-Federal-Budget,OMB-A11,edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Funding documents and cost stages}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Reimbursable vs direct cite.}] Reimbursable work charges costs to the \ac{nwcf} corpus and then bills the customer; direct cite (commercial contracts only) charges the customer's line of accounting directly~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Cost stages.}] Committed (funding document received), obligated (contract awarded), costed (work performed/material received)~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Carryover}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Definition.}] Dollar value of reimbursable work ordered and funded but not yet completed at \ac{fy} end~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11B-Ch3}.
  \item[\textbf{Formula.}] Carryover = Available balance (cash) + commitments + obligations (costed transactions excluded)~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Rules.}] Some carryover is required to start the new \ac{fy}; excess carryover can trigger congressional marks and future budget reductions~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Work Requests (WR) (Economy Act Orders)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Specificity.}] Used to request routine or recurring day-to-day services within the Navy~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch3}.
  \item[\textbf{Timing.}] Work must be completed before expiration of funds cited~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch3}.
  \item[\textbf{Restrictions.}] Cannot be used to fund the basic mission of the performing activity~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch3}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Project orders (PO)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Specificity.}] Description of work and period of performance must be specific and produce a tangible end product~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch2}.
  \item[\textbf{Timing.}] Work may extend past fund expiration but not beyond cancellation; \acp{po} cannot be issued solely to extend funds and scope cannot expand after expiration~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch2}.
  \item[\textbf{51/49 rule.}] At least 51\% of reimbursable work must be performed in-house; no more than 49\% can be out-house~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch2}.
  \item[\textbf{Restrictions.}] \acp{po} are reimbursable only and limited to \ac{dod} activities; they extend the cited funds beyond expiration and carry additional restrictions~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025,DoDFMR-Vol11A-Ch2}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Other funding documents}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{MIPR.}] Transfers funds between military organizations for supplies or services; can be accepted on a direct cite or reimbursable basis~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{IPR.}] Used when requiring and performing activities are from different departments (outside \ac{dod}); cannot cite the \ac{po} statute but may cite \ac{wr} or other statutory authorities~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{FS Form 7600B.}] Treasury G-Invoicing form for reimbursable \acp{igt}; applicable to manual or electronic submissions and not limited to \ac{wr} or \ac{po} authorities~\autocite{edo-3-1-5-field-activity-fm-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item What is the board trap in \ac{nwcf}? \textbf{Answer:} Treating the activity as profit-seeking or directly
  appropriated for customer work; it is reimbursable and aims to break even through stabilized rates and \ac{aor}
  management.
  \item What is the WR versus PO distinction? \textbf{Answer:} Work requests are Economy Act service orders tied to the
  cited funds' availability; project orders require a specific tangible end product and cannot be used only to extend
  expiring funds.
  \item What should an EDO check before accepting funds? \textbf{Answer:} Purpose, time, amount, customer authority, work
  specificity, and whether the document is direct-cite, reimbursable, WR, PO, MIPR, IPR, or G-Invoicing.
\end{itemize}
