\section{Career Topics}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{FITREP fundamentals.}] Strong \ac{fitrep} counseling, honest feedback, and clear ranking
  narratives are essential because evaluations are a primary source for officer personnel-management decisions~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,BUPERSINST-1610-10H-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{OSR/PSR focus.}] Selection boards review the \ac{osr} and \ac{psr} for record continuity,
  competitive ranking, and trends in trait averages and breakout language, but those summaries must be consistent with the \ac{ompf}~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,MyNavyHR-Officer-Record-Update-2026}.
  \item[\textbf{Board procedures.}] Administrative and statutory boards follow different authorities, but all
  boards use precepts, convening orders, and structured screening processes to select the best qualified~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Promotion context.}] \ac{dopma} governs promotion zones and selection cohorts, while recent
  changes emphasize merit and technical performance over seniority~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Record ownership.}] Officers are responsible for reviewing records, correcting gaps, and
  preparing for boards that are largely staffed by \ac{url} members~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{FITREP Counseling and Performance Signals}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mid-term counseling.}] Mid-term counseling around the six-month point improves performance,
  clarifies expectations, and provides the feedback needed to improve \ac{fitrep} outcomes~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,BUPERSINST-1610-10H-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Reporting senior expectations.}] Officers should ask their \ac{rs} how rankings and trait
  grades are determined, where they stand among peers, and what is needed to improve performance
  narratives~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Competitive signals.}] Boards look for high rankings, trait averages above \ac{rsca}, and
  progression toward the right over time, with strong breakout language in \ac{fitrep} narratives~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{OSR/PSR and Record Continuity}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Board view.}] The \ac{osr} and \ac{psr} are core summary records used with the \ac{ompf}, so
  officers should regularly review all three for accuracy and continuity~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,MyNavyHR-Officer-Record-Update-2026}.
  \item[\textbf{Letters to the board.}] Letters can clarify missing or late information, but should be concise
  and submitted before the board convenes so record briefers can address them~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Photos and files.}] Officer photographs remain part of the \ac{ompf}, even though selection
  boards no longer display them during review~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,MyNavyHR-Officer-Record-Update-2026}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Selection Board Types and Authorities}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Administrative vs. statutory.}] Administrative boards are convened by policy while statutory
  boards are convened by \ac{secnav} and governed by law, with membership and procedures set by
  statute and instruction~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,MyNavyHR-Selection-Boards-2026}.
  \item[\textbf{DOPMA framework.}] \ac{dopma} standardizes promotion rules, establishes grade ceilings,
  and drives selection board cohorts and separation timelines~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Zones and lineal numbers.}] Promotion zones are determined by projected requirements and
  lineal seniority, with annual updates published for in-zone and below-zone eligibility~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Board Processes}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Recorders and briefers.}] Recorders check record continuity and briefers annotate \ac{osr}/\ac{psr}
  entries so board members can quickly assess records during deliberations~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Voting flow.}] Boards brief records, vote confidence levels, and apply ``crunch'' reviews to
  refine selections until authorized numbers are met~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Promotion Board Changes and Merit Reorder}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Merit emphasis.}] Current promotion-board convening orders may authorize merit-based reordering for officers of particular merit, so treat the exact criteria and percentage as board-specific rather than a generic seniority override~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,MyNavyHR-FY26-Staff-Convening-Order-Merit-Reorder}.
  \item[\textbf{EDO merit signals.}] Sustained top performance, demanding \ac{edo} assignments, and proven
  leadership roles are valued indicators for merit reorder decisions~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Lessons Learned}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Own the record.}] Review your record regularly, verify \ac{fitrep} continuity, and ensure awards,
  education, and qualifications are current before board eligibility~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Know the audience.}] Most selection board members are \ac{url} officers; write \ac{fitrep}
  narratives and accomplishments so they resonate with that audience~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Seek mentorship.}] Engage detailers and senior \acp{edo} early to identify and address gaps
  before selection board reviews~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \ac{fitrep} counseling and breakout language drive board perceptions of performance and potential~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item The \ac{osr}/\ac{psr} and \ac{ompf} must agree, so record continuity matters~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025,MyNavyHR-Officer-Record-Update-2026}.
  \item \ac{dopma} sets promotion zones, cohorts, and baseline promotion rules~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item Selection boards use structured brief, vote, and crunch processes to reach authorized selects~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
  \item Most board members are \ac{url} officers, so \ac{fitrep} narratives must speak to that audience~\autocite{edo-5-2-3-career-topics-2025}.
\end{itemize}
