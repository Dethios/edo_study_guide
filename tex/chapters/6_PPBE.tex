\section{PPBE (Planning, Programming, Budgeting, and Execution)}

\subsection{Role of the PPBE process}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Why PPBE exists.}] The \ac{dod} uses \ac{ppbe} to identify fiscal needs and allocate resources across the Services and \ac{dod} agencies.
  \item[\textbf{Scale.}] Annual budget exceeds \$885B and supports about 3 million active duty and civilian employees.
  \item[\textbf{Balancing act.}] Weighs competing requirements for limited funds while reconciling internal priorities and external direction from \ac{omb} and Congress.
  \item[\textbf{Output.}] The end product is the \ac{dod} portion of the President's Budget.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{PPBE overview}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Planning.}] Predict the strategic environment and define the capabilities it requires.
  \item[\textbf{Programming.}] Allocate resources to missions and integrate programs to minimize shortfalls and duplication.
  \item[\textbf{Budgeting.}] Produce an annual budget for Congress that links missions to requested funding.
  \item[\textbf{Execution.}] Operate in the real world and make adjustments.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{Future Years Defense Program (FYDP)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Definition.}] The \ac{dod}'s internal database for tracking resource allocations.
  \item[\textbf{Navy access.}] The Navy uses the \ac{pbis} for controlled access.
  \item[\textbf{Time horizon.}] Prior Year (PY), Current Year (CY), Budget Year (BY), plus four out-years (POM) and additional force-structure-only years.
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Track \ac{dod} funds, force structure, and personnel strength throughout PPBE.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{FYDP structure}
The \ac{fydp} lets users identify, sort, and display \ac{dod} plans by three dimensions: Major Force Program, appropriation, and component/agency~\autocite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}.

\subsubsection{Major Force Programs}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Twelve MFPs.}] The \ac{fydp} organizes data into 12 \acp{mfp}.
  \item[\textbf{Combat vs support.}] Six \acp{mfp} are combat force programs and six are support programs.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsubsection{Appropriations}
Appropriation categories are the major funding classes by which Congress provides funds for all federal agencies, including the \ac{dod}~\autocite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}.

\subsubsection{Component or agency}
The \ac{fydp} tracks funding for each military service and other \ac{dod} agencies; each data set may include multiple appropriations and \acp{mfp}~\autocite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}.

\subsubsection{Program elements}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{PE codes.}] Resources are assigned to a unique \ac{mfp} using \ac{pe} codes.
  \item[\textbf{Granularity.}] \acp{pe} can be as narrow as a single platform or as broad as strategic planning.
  \item[\textbf{Multiple appropriations.}] A single \ac{pe} can include multiple appropriation types (for example, procurement plus \ac{omn} for sustainment).
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{Planning phase}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Lead.}] Led by \ac{usdp} with inputs from \ac{cjcs}, \acp{ccmd}, and \ac{osd} staff.
  \item[\textbf{Key products.}] \ac{cpr} and \ac{dpg}.
  \item[\textbf{Focus.}] Threat versus capability gaps, strategy updates, and guidance for programming and budgeting.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsubsection{Planning phase key documents}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{CPR.}] \ac{cjcs} coordinates with the \ac{jroc} and \acp{ccmd} to identify priorities for \ac{dpg} input; signed by \ac{cjcs}.
  \item[\textbf{DPG.}] Drafted by \ac{usdp} and signed by \ac{secdef}; provides force-structure and resource priorities for program development and is the end product of the planning phase.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{Programming phase}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{POM development.}] Each department and \ac{dod} agency creates a \ac{pom} that allocates resources across programs over five years starting with the budget year.
  \item[\textbf{OSW program review.}] \ac{osd} conducts a program review; \ac{cape} adjudicates issues and \ac{cjcs} signs the \ac{cpa}.
  \item[\textbf{Decision.}] Deputy \ac{secdef} issues a \ac{pdm} approving POMs as modified by the review.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{Navy programming process}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Top-down fiscal guidance.}] Fiscal controls flow from \ac{omb} to \ac{osd}, \ac{don}, \ac{opnav}, resource sponsors, and then programs.
  \item[\textbf{Bottom-up POM.}] Units propose resources; \ac{n8} integrates sponsor submissions into the Navy tentative \ac{pom}; \ac{don} integrates Navy and USMC inputs and \ac{secnav} signs the \ac{don} \ac{pom}.
  \item[\textbf{Issues.}] Proposed changes from previous funding levels are tracked and adjudicated as issues.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsubsection{FMB and Fiscal Management Division (N82)}
\label{subsec:fmb}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Organization distinction.}] \ac{fmb} refers to the Deputy Assistant Secretary of the Navy for Budget office/title; \ac{n82} is OPNAV's Fiscal Management Division. A War Department flag-officer assignment notice identifies the same officer as both DASN Budget (FMB) and Director, Fiscal Management Division, N82, which is why board discussions often say ``FMB/N82'' as a combined budget lane~\autocite{WarGov-Flag-Officer-Assignments-FMB-N82-2020}.
  \item[\textbf{Role.}] The FMB/N82 lane is the Navy budgeting and fiscal-control arm, responsible for turning the Navy program (\ac{pom}) into an executable budget and managing its execution.
  \item[\textbf{\ac{ppbe} space.}] FMB/N82 operates in the \textbf{Budgeting + Execution} phases, distinct from N80's programming role.
  \item[\textbf{Budget formulation.}] Translates the first budget year of the \ac{pom} into the \ac{bes}, aligns Navy funding with \ac{osd}/\ac{omb} guidance, and prepares and defends the Navy budget through \ac{osd} review, \ac{omb} review, and Congressional submission.
  \item[\textbf{Fiscal controls and execution.}] Manages appropriation execution, enforces obligation/expenditure controls and reprogramming rules, and tracks whether programs are spending as planned or deviating from budget intent.
  \item[\textbf{Budget defense.}] Leads Navy response to \acp{pbd} from \ac{osd} and Congressional marks by drafting reclamas, building business case and operational impact arguments, and coordinating with resource sponsors (N9, etc.).
  \item[\textbf{Integration with resource sponsors.}] Works with N9 sponsors (N95--N99, N9I, N94) to refine budget submissions, adjust funding based on marks, and ensure affordability within constraints.
  \item[\textbf{Appropriation structure.}] Operates heavily in appropriation categories (RDT\&E, Procurement, O\&M, MILPERS, etc.), \acp{pe}, and budget exhibits.
  \item[\textbf{Key distinction.}] Programming (N80) = capability + trade space; Budgeting (FMB/N82) = appropriation control + executable funding.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025,OPNAVINST4700-7N,WarGov-Flag-Officer-Assignments-FMB-N82-2020}

\subsubsection{FMB responsibilities (high-yield list)}
For board answers, the FMB/N82 budget lane is responsible for:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Budget formulation (\ac{bes})
  \item Budget justification and defense (\ac{osd}, \ac{omb}, Congress)
  \item Managing fiscal controls and execution
  \item Responding to \acp{pbd} and Congressional marks
  \item Ensuring compliance with appropriation law and policy
  \item Translating Navy priorities into executable funding
  \item Monitoring execution performance
\end{itemize}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025,OPNAVINST4700-7N}

The flow is: Planning produces the \ac{dpg}; the \ac{dpg} tells Components what strategic priorities and fiscal constraints to program against; Navy sponsors propose requirements and resources; N8/N80 integrates them into the Navy \ac{pom}; CAPE reviews the \ac{pom}; and \ac{cjcs} separately provides the \ac{cpa} on strategy, risk, and combatant-command alignment. In parallel or near-parallel, FMB/N82 turns the budget year of the program into the \ac{bes}; \ac{ousdc} and \ac{omb} review the \ac{bes} for pricing, phasing, funding policy, execution, and defendability. Programming decisions become \acp{pdm}; budget decisions become \acp{pbd}. If a Service strongly disagrees with a draft budget decision, it submits a reclama; if still unresolved and important enough, the issue may become an \ac{mbi} before final \ac{pb} lock.

\subsection{Programming phase key documents}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{POM.}] Time-phased resource allocation to support the \ac{dpg}; reviewed for compliance with \ac{dpg} and the \ac{nms}; signed by Service Secretaries (\ac{secnav} for the Navy).
  \item[\textbf{PDM.}] \ac{secdef} direction that summarizes decisions and approves the component \ac{pom} as modified by the program and budget review; issued by the Deputy \ac{secdef}.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{Budgeting phase key events and documents}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Budget Estimate Submission.}] Services submit \ac{bes} or Budget Change Proposals in appropriation format; signed by Service Secretaries.
  \item[\textbf{Budget hearings.}] Joint \ac{osd} and \ac{omb} hearings with advance questions on programs.
  \item[\textbf{Program Budget Decisions.}] \acp{pbd} direct budget adjustments; Services prepare reclamas; signed by the Deputy \ac{secdef}.
  \item[\textbf{Major Budget Issues.}] \ac{mbi} provide the last chance to raise issues to \ac{osd} before the \ac{pb}.
  \item[\textbf{President's Budget.}] \ac{pb} is submitted to Congress (nominally the first Monday in February).
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsubsection{PBDs and reclamas}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{PBD origin.}] Issued by USW(FM\&C) analysts after review of the \ac{pom}/\ac{bes} and budget hearing responses.
  \item[\textbf{Reclama.}] Draft \acp{pbd} are vetted for comment; affected parties submit reclamas with operational impacts and business case justification.
  \item[\textbf{Final decision.}] USW(FM\&C) recommends to the Deputy \ac{secdef}, who signs the final \acp{pbd}.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}

\subsection{Execution phase}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Assess programs of record against actual performance and adjust plans and budgets to minimize near-term financial risk.
  \item[\textbf{Activities.}] Mid-year review, reprogramming, supplemental appropriations, and financial closeout.
\end{description}
~\srcCite{edo-3-1-1-ppbe-2025}
