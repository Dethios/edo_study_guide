\section{Naval Warfare Centers}
Warfare centers provide critical technical expertise to ensure current and future program success and fleet readiness~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.

\subsection{Roles of the Warfare Centers}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Industry will not do.}] Not profitable or accepts too much liability.
  \item[\textbf{Industry should not do.}] Technical authority and certification work (for example, shock tests).
  \item[\textbf{Industry cannot do.}] Specialized facilities such as ranges and unique equipment.
\end{description}

\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Program success.}] Provide technical advice and engineering leadership to make naval technical programs successful.
  \item[\textbf{Capability development.}] Help determine and develop the capabilities the Navy and Marine Corps need as trusted technical advisors.
  \item[\textbf{Quality and safety.}] Verify quality, safety, and effectiveness of platforms and systems through rigorous systems engineering.
  \item[\textbf{Urgent needs.}] Design, develop, and field solutions for urgent operational fleet needs.
  \item[\textbf{Warfighter bridge.}] Translate warfighter needs into technical requirements.
\end{description}

\subsection{Reporting relationships}
Do not draw warfare centers as a separate acquisition chain. For a board answer,
start with the parent command: \ac{navsea} has \ac{nswc} and \ac{nuwc};
\ac{navwar} has the bicoastal \acp{niwc}; \ac{navair} has \ac{nawcad} and
\ac{nawcwd}; \ac{navfac} has \ac{exwc}; and \ac{onr} includes \ac{nrl} inside
the Naval Research Enterprise. Then explain the customer/product relationship:
\acp{peo}, \acp{pm}, Fleet users, resource sponsors, other \acp{syscom},
joint partners, allies, and industry partners consume technical products,
engineering services, test results, certifications, in-service engineering,
sustainment support, and \ac{rdte} outcomes~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024,NAVSEA-Warfare-Centers-2026,NAVSEA-Field-Activities-2026,navwar-who-we-are-2026,NAVAIR-NAWCAD-2026,NAVAIR-NAWCWD-About-2026,NAVFAC-EXWC-Home-2026,ONR-Organization-2026}.

\subsection{Warfare center missions}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Full-spectrum role.}] The Navy's full-spectrum \ac{rdte}, engineering, and fleet support centers for platforms, vehicles, systems, and facilities associated with a particular warfare area.
  \item[\textbf{NAVWAR.}] \ac{c4i} systems and information assurance.
  \item[\textbf{NAVAIR.}] Aircraft and aircraft systems.
  \item[\textbf{NAVSEA.}] Ships and ship systems.
  \item[\textbf{NAVFAC.}] Facilities.
\end{description}

\subsection{NAVWAR activities}
\subsubsection{NIWC Pacific}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Navy's premier \ac{rdte} laboratory supporting \ac{c4isr}; supports the development, acquisition, and fielding of leading-edge \ac{c4isr} systems for Navy, joint, and allied customers~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Locations.}] See \Fig{\ref{fig:niwc-pac-locations}}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NIWC Atlantic}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Primary \ac{navwar} provider for joint and homeland security \ac{c4isr} solutions; developer and employer of life-cycle logistics support solutions in a web-enabled portal environment; Navy provider of critical engineering and acquisition expertise for Navy and joint commands; rapid integrator of \ac{c4isr} technology providing interoperability to the Navy, federal agencies, and the joint warfighter~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Locations.}] See \Fig{\ref{fig:niwc-lant-locations}}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NAVWAR Space Field Activity (NSFA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Background.}] Established to coordinate naval space research activities within the \ac{nro}~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provide line management staffing of naval space research at \ac{nro}~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Responsibilities.}] Facilitate interaction between the \ac{don} and \ac{nro}; coordinate all naval space research, development, and acquisition functions and activities~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{NAVAIR warfare centers and divisions}
\subsubsection{NAWC Aircraft Division (NAWCAD)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Patuxent River, MD; detachments at Lakehurst, NJ and Orlando, FL~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] The Navy's principal \ac{rdte}, engineering, and fleet support activity for naval aircraft, engines, avionics, aircraft support systems, aircraft launch and recovery, and ship, shore, and air operation~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Airworthiness and engineering analysis; trainers and training development; Test Pilot School; test ranges; carrier support and aircraft launch and recovery equipment (ALRE); propulsion systems~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NAWC Weapons Division (NAWCWD)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] China Lake and Point Mugu, CA~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provide Navy and Marine Corps warriors with effective, affordable, integrated warfare systems and life-cycle support to ensure battlespace dominance~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Perform \ac{rdte}, logistics, and in-service support for guided missiles, free-fall weapons, targets, support equipment, crew systems, and electronic warfare; operate the Navy's western land and sea range test and evaluation complex; integrate weapons and avionics on tactical aircraft~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{NAVSEA warfare centers and divisions}
\subsubsection{Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Headquarters at Washington, DC~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provide the right technology, the right capabilities, and the specialized research and development facilities to support all aspects of surface warfare~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Ships and ship systems; surface ship combat systems; littoral warfare systems; Navy strategic weapon systems; ordnance; homeland and force protection; surface warfare logistics and maintenance; force level warfare systems~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Carderock Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] West Bethesda, MD~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provides research, engineering, modeling, and testing for ships and ship systems for advanced platforms, analytics, and cost-conscious performance improvements~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Hydromechanics (hull form, resistance measurement, prop design, maneuvering, seakeeping analysis); surface and submarine survivability; ship and submarine electromagnetic and optical signatures~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Early life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Corona Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Corona, CA~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Gauges the warfighting capacity of ships and aircraft, from unit to battlegroup level, by assessing the suitability of design, the performance of equipment and weapons, and the adequacy of training~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Fleet exercise assessments; weapons and combat systems performance analysis; missile flight analysis; data collection, processing, reduction and display; trend and failure pattern analysis; test systems availability assessment; metrology systems engineering; material readiness assessment~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Entire life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Crane Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Crane, IN~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Advance multi-domain systems within electronic warfare, strategic systems hardware, and special and expeditionary warfare; conduct \ac{rdte}, acquisition, and in-service engineering across the entire life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Electronic warfare systems \ac{rdte}, acquisition, and sustainment; special warfare and expeditionary system hardware; strategic systems hardware; infrared countermeasures, pyrotechnic \ac{rdte}, and life-cycle support; advanced electronics and energy systems; sensors and surveillance systems~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Entire life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Dahlgren Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Dahlgren, VA~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Research, develop, test and evaluate, analyze, engineer, integrate, and certify complex naval warfare systems related to surface warfare, strategic systems, combat and weapons systems~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Surface ship combat systems software engineering (theater air defense, Aegis combat system, DDG~1000); certifies each strike group for interoperability; develop and certify targeting and fire control systems for Trident submarines~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Early life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Indian Head Explosive Ordnance Disposal Technology Division (IHEODTECHD)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Indian Head, MD~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Research, develop, test, evaluate, manufacture, and provide in-service support of energetics and energetic systems; provide warfighters with technology to detect, locate, access, identify, render safe, recover, exploit, and dispose of explosive threats~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Energetics research, detonation science, chemical and physical characterization; weapons product development; ordnance test and evaluation; \ac{navsea} lead for weapon and combat systems packaging, handling, storage, and transportation; support the Joint \ac{eod} technician; counter radio-controlled improvised explosive device electronic warfare (CREW) science and technology; only US source and \ac{dod} facility for many types of propellant and high-energy chemicals; one of two solvent-less extrusion plants in the US; model and scale-up warheads~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Entire life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Panama City Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Panama City, FL~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Conduct \ac{rdte} and in-service support of mine countermeasure systems, naval sea mine systems, naval special warfare systems, diving and life support systems, amphibious and expeditionary maneuver warfare systems, and other systems that occur primarily in coastal (littoral) regions~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Mine warfare; \ac{lcs} mission packages; expeditionary maneuver warfare; assault craft vehicles; diving and life support systems; unmanned systems (air and ground); chemical and biological individual protection; special warfare~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Entire life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Philadelphia Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Philadelphia, PA~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provides \ac{rdte}, fleet support, and in-service engineering for surface and undersea vehicle \ac{hme} and propulsors; provides logistics \ac{rnd}~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Surface and submarine machinery \ac{rnd} (power systems, ship automation control, dynamics and silencing, shipboard energy availability and conservation, and electrical machinery integration); machinery \ac{isea} for propulsion, electric and mechanical systems; Integrated Propulsion System development; control systems and \ac{hme} cybersecurity~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Post-production~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NSWC Port Hueneme Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Port Hueneme, CA~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provide test and evaluation, in-service engineering, and integrated logistics support for surface warfare combat systems and ordnance of the Navy surface fleet~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Surface missile launcher systems; UNREP test site and fleet support; Self Defense Test Ship; surface ship combat systems; land-based and at-sea \ac{te}~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Post-production~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{Naval Undersea Warfare Center (NUWC)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Headquarters in Newport, RI~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] The Navy's full-spectrum \ac{rdte}, engineering, and fleet support center for submarines, autonomous underwater systems, and offensive and defensive weapons systems associated with undersea warfare~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Undersea warfare modeling and analysis; submarine combat and combat control systems; surface ship and submarine sonar systems; submarine electronic warfare; submarine unique on-board communication systems and communication nodes; undersea ranges; submarine electromagnetic, electro-optic and non-acoustic effects reconnaissance, and search and tracking systems; undersea vehicle active and passive signatures (except \ac{hme}); submarine vulnerability and survivability (except \ac{hme}); torpedoes and torpedo countermeasures~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NUWC Keyport Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Keyport, WA~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provide advanced technical capabilities for test and evaluation, in-service engineering, maintenance and industrial base support, fleet material readiness, and obsolescence management for undersea warfare~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Test and evaluation and training (\ac{nutec}, 3D undersea warfare tracking range, range alternatives); life-cycle systems supportability (fielded equipment, systems, subsystems); fleet material readiness (material support, modernization, prototype development, testing); depot level torpedo repair and product proofing~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Post-production~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{NUWC Newport Division}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location.}] Newport, RI~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Provide the technical foundation which enables the conceptualization, \ac{rdte}, fielding, modernization, and maintenance of systems that ensure the Navy's undersea superiority~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Product-oriented research and advanced development; undersea warfare modeling and analysis; \acp{uuv}; torpedoes, torpedo launchers, and torpedo countermeasures (submarine and surface) as in-service engineering agent and design agent; operational testing support (\ac{optevfor}, \ac{cssqt}) -- Atlantic ranges; operate \ac{autec}; submarine cybersecurity~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Early life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Warfare center organization}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Leadership.}] Warfare Center Board of Directors; \ac{nswc} and \ac{nuwc} Commander and Executive Director; \ac{nswc} and \ac{nuwc} staff~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Division leadership.}] Each \ac{nswc}/\ac{nuwc} division has a Commanding Officer, Technical Director, and Senior Civilian~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Dam Neck Activity.}] \ac{nswc} Dahlgren Dam Neck Activity includes a Commanding Officer and Senior Civilian; O-5 command~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{NSLC.}] Naval Sea Logistics Center (Mechanicsburg) reports within the warfare center enterprise~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Office of Naval Research and Naval Research Laboratory}
\subsubsection{Office of Naval Research (ONR)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Leadership.}] Typically headed by a Navy two-star; mostly civilian staff; reports to \ac{asnrdanda}~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Research channels.}] Science and technology; Naval Research Laboratory; \ac{onr} Global (fleet, international, and \ac{sandt} community information sharing); Naval Reserve \ac{sandt} Program; Commercial Technology Transition Office for rapid transitions~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsubsection{Naval Research Laboratory (NRL)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Location and reporting.}] Washington, DC; headed by an O-6; reports to \ac{onr}~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] The Navy's full-spectrum corporate laboratory to conduct broad-based multidisciplined scientific research and advanced technology development for maritime applications of new and improved materials, techniques, equipment, systems, and ocean, atmospheric, and space sciences~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technical leadership areas.}] Primary in-house research for physical, engineering, space, and environmental sciences; broad-based exploratory and advanced development for anticipated Navy needs; broad multidisciplinary support to the Naval Warfare Centers~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Life-cycle focus.}] Early life-cycle~\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Coursebook cue: warfare-center customers and products}

Coursebook 2.1.4 expects students to recognize that naval warfare centers are
not single-customer laboratories.  Their customer base includes NAVSEA and
NAVAIR program offices, fleet type commanders, resource sponsors, other SYSCOMs,
joint and allied partners, and field activities that need technical authority,
test, evaluation, in-service engineering, fleet support, certification, and
life-cycle problem solving.  The product is often not just hardware; it can be a
technical decision, test result, certification basis, engineering agent action,
fleet advisory, modernization package, or sustainment recommendation
\autocite{edo-2-1-4-warfare-centers-2024}.

\paragraph{Board cue.}
Answer with customers plus products: PEOs and fleet customers buy decisions,
data, certification, test, engineering support, modernization, and sustainment
outcomes.
