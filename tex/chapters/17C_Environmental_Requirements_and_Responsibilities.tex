\section{Environmental Requirements and Responsibilities}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{NEPA foundation.}] \ac{nepa} is a procedural statute requiring the \ac{dod} to consider environmental impacts for major federal actions, involve the public, and document decisions~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{2025 update.}] Executive Order~14154 directed \ac{ceq} to rescind 40~CFR~1500; \ac{ceq} rescinded its regulations effective April~11,~2025 and the \ac{dod} issued new \ac{nepa} procedures on June~30,~2025~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Review levels.}] \ac{catex}, \ac{nepaea}, and \ac{eis} drive outcomes such as \ac{fonsi} or \ac{rod}, with public involvement when appropriate~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{OCONUS planning.}] \ac{nepa} does not apply \ac{oconus}; Executive Order~12114 and 32~CFR~187 require a \ac{nepa}-like analysis for significant overseas impacts~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{ESOH integration.}] \ac{esoh} compliance is embedded in acquisition; \ac{peshe} documentation is required for all \ac{acat} milestone reviews and covers compliance, safety, hazards, pollution prevention, and \ac{nepa} status~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Procedural statute.}] For major federal actions affecting the human environment, \ac{nepa} requires the \ac{dod} to consider environmental impacts, involve the public, seek less damaging alternatives, and document decisions~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{PM responsibility.}] Program managers should design systems to minimize negative impacts on human health and the environment~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Compliance schedule.}] \ac{dod} policy requires a \ac{nepa} compliance schedule documented in the \ac{peshe} and integrated into the program schedule~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{What Is New with NEPA}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Executive Order 14154.}] Directed \ac{ceq} to rescind 40~CFR~1500 and provide new implementation guidance; \ac{ceq} rescinded its regulations effective April~11,~2025~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Agency actions.}] Federal agencies were instructed to repeal existing CFR \ac{nepa} rules and develop agency-specific procedures~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{DoD implementation.}] The \ac{dod} released new \ac{nepa} procedures on June~30,~2025 to standardize compliance across components and maintain continuity in the review process~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Component rules.}] As of June~30,~2025, component \ac{nepa} regulations in the CFR were repealed and replaced by enterprise procedures; \ac{don} will issue supplemental guidance~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{DoN statement.}] \ac{don} reaffirmed its commitment to \ac{nepa} compliance, transparency, and stakeholder communication under the new procedures~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{When NEPA Review Is Not Required}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No final agency action, statutory exemption, or legal conflict with other requirements~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Congress limits \ac{dod} discretion by statute or another law satisfies \ac{nepa} requirements~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Actions that are not major federal actions or have minimal federal involvement or funding~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Issuance of the \ac{dod} \ac{nepa} procedures themselves~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Determining the Level of NEPA Review}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{CATEX.}] Check for existing \ac{catex} and apply \ac{dod} or other agency \ac{catex} if applicable; consider revising or creating a \ac{catex} if needed~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Environmental Assessment.}] If reasonably foreseeable effects are not likely significant or are unknown, prepare an \ac{nepaea}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Environmental Impact Statement.}] If impacts are likely significant, prepare an \ac{eis}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{DoW as a Single Federal Agency for CATEX}
\ac{dod} and its components are treated as a single federal agency for \ac{catex} purposes, regardless of which component established the exclusion~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.

\subsection{Other Laws, Statutes, and Executive Orders Integrated with NEPA}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Water and air statutes: Rivers and Harbors Act of 1899, Wild and Scenic Rivers Act, Safe Drinking Water Act, Coastal Zone Management Act, \ac{cwa}, and \ac{caa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Species and wildlife protections: Magnuson-Stevens Fishery Conservation and Management Act, \ac{esa}, Bald and Golden Eagle Protection Act, and Migratory Bird Treaty Act~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Land and agriculture: Executive Order~11990 (Wetlands), Executive Order~11988 (Floodplain Management), and the Farmland Protection Policy Act~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Cultural and tribal resources: \ac{nhpa}, Archaeological Resources Protection Act, Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation Act, and American Indian Religious Freedom Act~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Chemical and hazardous waste: \ac{tsca}, \ac{rcra}, and \ac{cercla}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Human health: Executive Order~13045 (Protection of Children from Environmental Health and Safety Risks)~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{NEPA Process Outcomes}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{CATEX record.}] A categorical exclusion may produce a record of \ac{catex}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{EA outcome.}] An \ac{nepaea} may result in a \ac{fonsi} when impacts are not significant; public involvement occurs as appropriate~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{EIS outcome.}] An \ac{eis} requires a notice of intent, scoping, draft and final \ac{eis} publication, public and agency comment, and concludes with a \ac{rod} and mitigation monitoring~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Environmental Planning Outside the United States}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{OCONUS.}] \ac{nepa} does not apply outside U.S. territories and territorial seas; Executive Order~12114 governs environmental effects abroad~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Implementation.}] \ac{dod} implementing regulation is 32~CFR~187; actions require a \ac{nepa}-like analysis when significant impacts are possible~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Environment, Safety, and Occupational Health (ESOH)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Scope.}] \ac{esoh} integrates environmental compliance, system safety, occupational safety and health, hazardous materials management, and pollution prevention~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Acquisition responsibility.}] Program managers must consider \ac{esoh} requirements throughout the life cycle when making design decisions~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{ESOH Requirements}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Compliance process.}] Poor \ac{esoh} planning can lead to civil or criminal penalties and program delays~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Execution.}] Compliance responsibility is assigned primarily to facility and installation managers and maintainers~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{PM focus.}] Program managers must integrate \ac{esoh} impacts as part of acquisition systems engineering~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{ESOH Law Summary}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Air pollution control: \ac{caa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Water pollution control: \ac{cwa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Hazardous waste: \ac{rcra} and \ac{cercla}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Chemical production and tracking: \ac{tsca} and \ac{epcra}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Endangered species and marine mammals: \ac{esa} and \ac{mmpa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Workplace safety: \ac{osha}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Environmental policy and planning: \ac{nepa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item Cultural resources: \ac{nhpa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Clean Air Act (CAA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Standards.}] Establishes national air quality standards for public health; amended in 1990~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{State implementation.}] States execute State Implementation Plans with permits, reporting, and operational controls for stationary and mobile sources~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Industrial controls.}] The \ac{epa} sets emission standards for aerospace manufacturing and shipbuilding and repair, emphasizing compliant materials and control devices~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Clean Water Act (CWA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Scope.}] Major federal legislation to control water pollution~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Permit program.}] Requires a National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permit program for point and non-point sources discharging to waters of the United States~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Examples.}] Point sources include ships and industrial facilities; non-point sources include highways and parking lots, and are the biggest challenge~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Clean Water Act Jurisdiction Update}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Wetlands test.}] The Supreme Court limited \ac{cwa} jurisdiction to wetlands with a continuous surface connection that makes them practically indistinguishable from waters of the United States~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Major federal legislation on hazardous waste management enacted in 1976 to protect health, conserve resources, reduce waste, and ensure sound disposal~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Cradle-to-grave control.}] Regulates generation, management, storage, transport, and disposal of hazardous waste with manifests and permits; applies to shore activities~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Aberdeen Three.}] Federal employees were convicted for improper storage, treatment, and disposal of hazardous waste under \ac{rcra}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Cleanup authority.}] Authorizes federal response to the release or threat of release of hazardous substances and focuses on old or inactive sites; costs are assigned to responsible parties~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Reporting.}] Releases of \ac{cercla} hazardous substances must be reported to the National Response Center~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Limitations.}] Petroleum, oils, and lubricants are covered by other programs, not \ac{cercla}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Common name.}] \ac{cercla} is commonly known as Superfund~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{EPA authority.}] Enables the \ac{epa} to track industrial chemicals and require reporting or testing; 2016 amendments streamlined listing and bans based on risk~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{DoN concerns.}] Regulates polychlorinated biphenyls, lead-based paint, asbestos, and radon~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Endangered Species Act (ESA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Protects threatened and endangered species and prohibits ``taking'' listed species~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Federal duty.}] Requires federal agencies to consult and affirmatively conserve ecosystems supporting protected species~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Marine Mammal Protection Act (MMPA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Maintains the health and stability of the marine ecosystem and limits ``take'' or harassment of marine mammals~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Authorizations.}] Activities such as sonar, sea trials, and weapons firings may require authorization from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration and \ac{nepa} analysis~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Cultural Resources}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{NHPA compliance.}] \ac{nhpa} requires federal agencies to assess impacts on historic property and consult with states and Native American tribes when actions affect historic characteristics~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act (EPCRA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Notifications.}] Requires immediate notification of state and local emergency planners and the facility's major claimant for releases over threshold levels~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Public information.}] Provides information on hazards associated with toxic chemical releases and encourages local emergency planning~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Fence line owner.}] The base commander files \ac{epcra} reports for the entire facility; releases that expose only personnel within facility boundaries do not require state or local notification~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Workplace and Employee Safety}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{OSH Act.}] The Occupational Safety and Health Act requires safe and healthful workplaces; \ac{osha} is the common reference~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Federal compliance.}] Federal agencies must comply with OSHA standards unless the Secretary of Labor approves an alternate standard; Executive Order~12196 applies~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Scope.}] Uniquely military equipment and operations are not covered, but DoW workplaces comparable to private industry are covered under 29~CFR~1960~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{DoW Policy}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{DoD 5000 series.}] Requires acquisition programs to prevent, mitigate, or remediate environmental damage; address issues early; and evaluate alternatives to reduce impacts across design, test, operation, and disposal~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Compliance duty.}] All \ac{dod} personnel, tenants, and contractors must comply with applicable federal, state, and local environmental requirements~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Defense Acquisition Program Requirements}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Lifecycle compliance.}] Program managers must ensure systems can be produced, tested, fielded, operated, trained, maintained, and disposed of in compliance with \ac{esoh} requirements~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Outcome focus.}] The goal is safeguarding the environment, reducing accidents, and protecting human health~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Programmatic ESOH Evaluation (PESHE)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{PM documentation.}] The \ac{peshe} documents the program process, the schedule for completing \ac{nepa} documentation, and \ac{esoh} risk status~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Requirement.}] Required for all \ac{acat} programs at milestone review and serves as a management and reporting tool for the \ac{pm}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Analysis areas.}] Environmental compliance, system safety and health, hazardous materials management, pollution prevention management, and \ac{nepa}~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item What environmental-related document must be updated before every milestone review~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}? \textbf{Answer:} \ac{peshe}.
  \item Which environmental law allows for discharge into the waters of the United States~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}? \textbf{Answer:} \ac{cwa}.
  \item What procedural law established the review process for major federal actions~\autocite{edo-3-3-7-environmental-requirements-2025}? \textbf{Answer:} \ac{nepa}.
\end{itemize}
