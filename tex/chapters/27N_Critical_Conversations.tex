\section{Critical Conversations}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] Critical conversations build a culture of awareness and candor that improves
  organizational performance and decision quality~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Bias and assumptions.}] Unchecked assumptions and cognitive biases distort judgment and
  undermine the \ac{grgb} mindset~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Emotional intelligence.}] \ac{eq} enables leaders to manage emotions, reduce conflict, and
  sustain trust during difficult discussions~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Preparation.}] Effective conversations require a safe space, clear intent, and understanding of
  human motivation and reactions~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Techniques.}] Use neutral tone, shared values, active listening, and solution-focused framing
  to keep dialogue constructive~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Principles and Rules of Engagement}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Consideration and candor.}] Leaders should engage with transparency, invite participation,
  and reinforce non-attribution discussion norms when appropriate~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Assumptions.}] Recognize that different perceptions can be valid; test assumptions early to
  prevent flawed conclusions~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Emotional Intelligence and Bias Awareness}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Emotional intelligence.}] \ac{eq} supports empathy, self-management, and constructive
  conflict resolution across teams~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Common biases.}] Halo and horn effects, confirmation bias, anchoring, and authority bias can
  distort reasoning and must be actively challenged~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Preparing for Difficult Conversations}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Create the conditions.}] Schedule a convenient time, establish a safe environment, and
  approach the discussion with openness and vulnerability~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Know the dynamics.}] Anticipate human motivation (heart, head, hand) and reactions (fight,
  flight, freeze, fawn, flop) to better guide the conversation~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Techniques for Constructive Dialogue}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Build trust.}] Use shared values, active listening, and respect to establish common ground
  and reduce defensiveness~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Use ``I'' statements.}] Frame concerns around observations and impact rather than blame to
  preserve dignity and encourage problem solving~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Focus on solutions.}] Discuss future actions, avoid assuming full resolution in one session,
  and reframe if the conversation becomes confrontational~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Critical conversations require candor, inclusivity, and explicit engagement rules~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item Assumptions and biases must be surfaced to prevent flawed decisions~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item \ac{eq} enables leaders to manage emotion and maintain team cohesion during conflict~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item Preparation and safe-space conditions increase the odds of productive dialogue~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
  \item Trust, empathy, and solution framing help move contentious issues toward resolution~\autocite{edo-5-2-9-critical-conversations-2025}.
\end{itemize}
