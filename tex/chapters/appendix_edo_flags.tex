\section{Current Billets and Recent Changes}
\textbf{Current as of 2026-10-20.} The roster baseline is the visually checked 23~September~2026 EDO School roster, not the superseded June list. Slides~49--50 identify twelve EDO flag officers: two vice admirals, three rear admirals, and seven rear admirals (lower half). Later public announcements update duties without automatically changing an officer's grade~\autocite[slides 49--50]{edo-2-1-1-command-structures-2025}.

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \ac{rdml} Peter D.\ Small is acting \ac{comnavsea}; \ac{vadm} James P.\ Downey relinquished command on 6~August. Rear Adm.\ Todd Weeks separately became interim PAE Industrial Operations. Those are now separate leadership assignments; the announcement does not identify a separate SEA~05 successor~\autocite{DVIDS-Small-NAVSEA-Aug2026,DVIDS-Weeks-PAEIO-Aug2026}.
  \item \ac{vadm} Douglas L.\ Williams relieved \ac{vadm} Johnny R.\ Wolfe Jr.\ as PAE Strategic Systems Programs on 12~June; this is a completed handoff~\autocite{DVIDS-SSP-Handoff-Jun2026}.
  \item \ac{radm} Casey Moton is acting PAE Maritime, confirmed in the portfolio's 30~September release. The September coursebook identifies \ac{rdml} Jonathan Jett-Parmer as acting NAVSEA vice commander~\autocite{PAE-Maritime-Moton-Sep2026,edo-2-1-1-command-structures-2025}.
  \item \ac{vadm} Seiko Okano performs \ac{asnrdanda} duties following the late-September transition. The September~23 slide's PMILDEP title predates that change~\autocite{ExecutiveGov-ASNRDA-Sep2026,DONCIO-Okano-PTDO-2026}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Departures and Grade Discipline}
Downey and Wolfe are no longer current incumbents in this roster. The Navy announced Downey's retirement following the August~6 handoff; Wolfe's June~12 event combined retirement and change of command~\autocite{DVIDS-Small-NAVSEA-Aug2026,DVIDS-SSP-Handoff-Jun2026}. Scott Brown is also absent from the September EDO roster: Capt.\ J.\ D.\ Crinklaw relieved him as Deputy PAE, Shipyard Operations, on 3~September. Do not retain Brown as the current SEA~04 flag incumbent~\autocite{DVIDS-Brown-Crinklaw-Sep2026}.

The twelve-officer count belongs to the September~23 coursebook roster; it is not an independently retrieved October personnel census. Keep RADM and RDML distinct, and do not infer a promotion from a biography's generic ``Rear Admiral'' heading. Weeks is named here to explain the PAE IO handoff, not added to the EDO count without EDO-community evidence. The separate June roster remains a historical source only.
