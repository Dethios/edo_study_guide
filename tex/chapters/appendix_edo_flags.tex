\section{Current Roster}
\noindent\textit{Current as of 2026-06-04. This appendix uses the June~2026 public Navy flag roster as the primary source for current grades and billets. Navy public biography headings that state ``Rear Admiral'' are not sufficient to distinguish \acs{radm}, RADM(sel), and \acs{rdml}; command biographies are used only to explain live title conflicts and transition cues~\autocite{navy-flag-roster-2026-06,SSP-Leadership-2026,navy-jett-parmer-2025,ssp-richman-2026}.}

\begin{description}[style=nextline, labelsep=0.5em, font=\bfseries]
  \item[\textbf{Scope.}] Includes active and reserve \ac{edo} flag officers who currently hold Navy or joint billets in service. Retired officers and aerospace engineering duty officers are excluded.
  \item[\textbf{Use.}] Treat this as the current board-prep roster. For broader billet density by location and community, use Appendix~\ref{app:edo-billet-distribution}.
\end{description}

\begin{description}[style=nextline, labelsep=0.5em, font=\bfseries]
  \item[\textbf{Roster interpretation notes.}] Scott Brown appears in the June~2026 roster under ``Rear Admiral (selectee)'' as ``RADM(S)''; this appendix lists him as RADM(sel), not \acs{rdml}, to preserve the board-relevant grade count. Richman's official \ac{ssp} leadership page uses ``Deputy Director, Regional Strike Systems,'' while the June~2026 roster uses ``Deputy Director for Hypersonics.'' Jett-Parmer's official Navy bio still lists ``Deputy Commander, Supervisor of Shipbuilding, Conversion and Repair,'' while the June~2026 roster lists ``Reserve Deputy, Naval Sea Systems Command.'' The Navy announced on 20~May~2026 that RADM Douglas Williams was confirmed for promotion to \ac{vadm} and would assume command as the 15th director of \ac{pae} \ac{ssp} in Jun~2026; the June~2026 roster still lists his current billet as Director for Test, Missile Defense Agency, while also ordering him to Director, Strategic Systems Programs. As of this 2026-06-04 refresh, the live \ac{ssp} leadership page still lists VADM Johnny Wolfe Jr.\ as Director for PAE SSP, so use the Wolfe/Williams item as a transition cue rather than a completed handoff unless a newer public change-of-command source is available~\autocite{navy-flag-roster-2026-06,SSP-Leadership-2026,DON-PAE-SSP-Williams-2026,navy-jett-parmer-2025,ssp-richman-2026}.
\end{description}
