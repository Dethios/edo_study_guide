\section{Current Roster}
\noindent\textit{Current as of 2026-06-04. This appendix uses the June~2026 public Navy flag roster as the primary source for current grades and billets. Navy public biography headings that state ``Rear Admiral'' are not sufficient to distinguish \acs{radm}, RADM(sel), and \acs{rdml}; command and PAE pages are used only to explain live title conflicts and transition cues~\autocite{navy-flag-roster-2026-06,SSP-Leadership-2026,DON-PAE-SSP-Williams-2026,PAE-Maritime-Homepage-2026}.}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}[style=nextline, labelsep=0.5em, font=\bfseries]
  \item[\textbf{Scope.}] Includes active and reserve \ac{edo} flag officers who currently hold Navy or joint billets in service. Retired officers and aerospace engineering duty officers are excluded.
  \item[\textbf{Use.}] Treat this as the current board-prep roster. For broader billet density by location and community, use Appendix~\ref{app:edo-billet-distribution}.
  \item[\textbf{Board count.}] Current board-speed count is 3~\acp{vadm}, 4~\acp{radm}, 1~RADM(sel), and 7~\acp{rdml}. Do not collapse \ac{radm} and \ac{rdml}, and do not add non-EDO PAE Maritime leaders such as RDML Andrew Biehn to the EDO roster~\autocite{navy-flag-roster-2026-06,PAE-Maritime-Homepage-2026}.
\end{description}
