\section{NAVWAR Enterprise}

\subsection{Team NAVWAR overview}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Organization.}] The \ac{navwar} enterprise consists of the \ac{navwar} systems command (Echelon~II), three affiliated \acp{peo} (\ac{peoc4i}, \ac{peodigital}, \ac{peomlb}), and three Echelon~III activities: \ac{niwclant}, \ac{niwcpac}, and \ac{nsfa}~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Team NAVWAR.}] More than 11{,}000 employees support fleet concentration areas and deployed operations worldwide~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Mission}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mission statement.}] Identify, develop, deliver, and sustain information warfighting capabilities and services that enable naval, joint, coalition, and other national missions from seabed to space, while providing research and development, systems engineering, test and evaluation, technical, in-service, and support services to \acp{peo} across program life cycles~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Echelon III activities and core competencies}
\subsubsection{NIWC Pacific}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Conduct research, development, prototyping, engineering, test and evaluation, installation, and sustainment of integrated \ac{c4isr}, cyber, and space systems across all warfighting domains~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Technology areas.}] Cyber operations; command and control communications and networks; production, installation, and in-service engineering; intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance and information operations; science and technology; marine mammal programs; autonomous unmanned systems~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.
  \item[\textbf{Key capability areas.}] Full life-cycle engineering for information warfare; cloud-based digital development with \ac{devsecops} and digital twins; rapid prototyping and delivery; \ac{ai}/data science; applied electromagnetics and photonics; offensive/defensive cyber; positioning, navigation, and timing labs; and unique test facilities including San Clemente Island and integrated data-link test beds~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.

\subsubsection{Naval Space Force Activity (NSFA)}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Mission.}] Lead the U.S.\ Navy's participation in the \ac{nro} by providing naval warfare and acquisition expertise, coordinating naval space \ac{rdte} with national reconnaissance programs, and enabling fleet use of national space capabilities~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.

  \item[\textbf{PEO Digital.}] \ac{don} enterprise-wide \ac{it} acquisition agent; portfolios include naval enterprise networks, special networks and intelligence mission applications, naval commercial cloud services, special access program networks, enterprise \ac{it} strategic sourcing, and Marine Corps supporting establishment systems~\autocite{edo-2-1-3-navwar-enterprise-2024}.

\subsubsection{PMW 120: Battlespace Awareness and Information Operations}
\label{ssubsec:pmw120}

\textbf{Program:} Distributed Common Ground System-Navy (DCGS-N)

\textbf{ACAT Status:} Increment 1 (ACAT IAC), Increment 2 (ACAT IAM)

PMW 120 focuses on creating and breaking ``kill webs'' for the warfighter by delivering integrated intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance (ISR), and electronic warfare capabilities. DCGS-N is the Navy's service component of the overarching DoD DCGS family of systems.

DCGS-N is designed to provide multiservice integration of ISR and targeting capabilities. DCGS-N Increment 1 (ACAT IAC) successfully consolidated various legacy intelligence analytical tools into an integrated computing environment, which has been fielded to force-level ships and key shore sites. DCGS-N Increment 2 (ACAT IAM) represents a generational leap in software architecture, providing robust, cross-domain data fusion and automated analytics workflows. Increment 2 serves as the primary bridge connecting naval operational sensors with the broader Joint and Intelligence Community (IC) enterprises.

\textbf{Strategic Insights:} The evolution of DCGS-N highlights a broader trend within NAVWAR ACAT I programs: the migration from hardware-heavy, monolithic deployments to agile, cloud-based software architectures. PMW 120's strategy involves transitioning from sustaining isolated systems to deploying afloat and ashore cloud environments. However, this transition introduces significant challenges in software growth, with instances of up to 30-percent increases in Source Lines of Code (SLOC) growth, requiring careful cost estimation and rigorous continuous testing.

\subsubsection{PMW 150: Naval Command and Control Systems}
\label{ssubsec:pmw150}

PMW 150 is the Navy's preeminent provider of operational and tactical command and control solutions. The office oversees a diverse portfolio that includes two massive ACAT I programs, highlighting the reality that combat efficacy and logistics readiness are operationally inseparable.

\textbf{Program 1: Global Command and Control System-Maritime (GCCS-M)}

\textbf{ACAT Status:} ACAT IAC

GCCS-M is the Navy's primary C2 program of record and the maritime iteration of the DoD's Joint C2 Family of Systems. It provides near-real-time tactical and operational situational awareness by generating the Common Operational Picture (COP) and Common Tactical Picture (CTP). GCCS-M processes and fuses tracks from ground, air, and maritime sensors, allowing commanders at Maritime Operations Centers (MOCs) ashore and aboard carrier strike groups to synchronize decentralized execution across the battlespace.

Over time, PMW 150's mandate has been to evolve GCCS-M into a more agile, service-oriented architecture (SOA). This evolution involves integrating Maritime Tactical Command and Control (MTC2) software applications, which dynamically plan, direct, monitor, and assess Distributed Maritime Operations (DMO) in real-time.

\textbf{Program 2: Naval Tactical Command Support System (NTCSS)}

\textbf{ACAT Status:} ACAT IAC

While GCCS-M commands the tactical fight, NTCSS commands the logistics required to sustain it. Designated as a Mission Essential \ac{mais} (\ac{acat} IAC), NTCSS is a comprehensive suite of software applications providing shipboard and aviation maintenance management, inventory control, and financial business management. Established in 1995 through the merger of legacy systems (SNAP, NALCOMIS, and MRMS), NTCSS is installed on vessels and ashore to support the unit commanding officer in tracking repair work packages, aviation repairables, and critical parts inventory.

The sustainment of the legacy NTCSS ACAT I baseline requires continuous configuration management and integration support. As PMW 150 shifts its focus toward modernized logistics frameworks, such as the Naval Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (N-MRO) and Naval Operational Supply System (NOSS) initiatives, maintaining the stability of the NTCSS backbone is paramount to prevent operational degradation and supply chain failures during the transition period.

\subsubsection{PMW 160: Tactical Networks}
\label{ssubsec:pmw160}

\textbf{Program:} Consolidated Afloat Networks and Enterprise Services (CANES)

\textbf{ACAT Status:} ACAT IAC / IAM

PMW 160 is tasked with providing mission-effective and cyber-resilient tactical networks to forces afloat. Its premier ACAT I program is CANES, a Chief of Naval Operations-directed initiative that serves as the indispensable core of the Navy's Information Warfare Platform.

CANES was initiated to collapse and replace five disparate legacy network systems (including ISNS, SubLAN, and CENTRIXS-M) into a single, consolidated afloat computing environment. It provides the necessary data transport, edge computing, voice, video, and systems management services across multiple security domains (NIPR, SIPR, SR, etc.) for the fleet. Achieving a Full Deployment Decision (FDD) in October 2015, CANES operates under ASN (RD\&A) oversight as an ACAT IAC program.

\textbf{Strategic Insights:} CANES represents arguably the most foundational enabler of maritime combat power within NAVWAR. Without the hardware and software infrastructure provided by CANES, advanced analytical applications developed by PMW 120 (DCGS-N) and PMW 150 (GCCS-M) simply cannot function afloat. Recognizing the pace of cyber threats, PMW 160 is aggressively driving CANES toward a DevSecOps framework, decoupling software development from hardware procurement and ensuring that commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) baselines are continuously evolved and patched through cyclical Service Pack updates.

\subsubsection{PMW 170: Communications and GPS Navigation}
\label{ssubsec:pmw170}

\textbf{Program:} Navy Multiband Terminal (NMT)

\textbf{ACAT Status:} ACAT IC

PMW 170 serves as the premier provider of resilient communications and assured Position, Navigation, and Timing (PNT). The office's flagship ACAT I program is the Navy Multiband Terminal (NMT).

NMT is the Navy's sole protected satellite communications terminal, providing critical, secure, survivable, and interoperable military satellite communications (MILSATCOM). NMT enables high-capacity communication by providing simultaneous access to a variety of existing and future satellite constellations, including Advanced Extremely High Frequency (AEHF), Wideband Global SATCOM (WGS), MILSTAR, Defense Satellite Communications System (DSCS), and the Enhanced Polar System (EPS).

\textbf{Strategic Insights:} In an era of great power competition, the electromagnetic spectrum is a highly contested domain. NMT is designed specifically to operate in anti-access/area-denial (A2/AD) environments, featuring extreme anti-jam, anti-scintillation, and low-probability-of-intercept/detection (LPI/LPD) capabilities using Extended Data Rate (XDR) waveforms. The strategic importance of this ACAT IC program cannot be overstated; it supports the President's Ballistic Missile Defense priorities and the National Command Authority by ensuring the uninterrupted flow of critical strategic data, even under sustained electronic and physical attack.

\subsubsection{PMW 205: Naval Enterprise Networks (PEO Digital)}
\label{ssubsec:pmw205}

\textbf{Program:} Next Generation Enterprise Network (NGEN) / NGEN-Recompete (NGEN-R)

\textbf{ACAT Status:} ACAT I / MAIS

Operating under the \ac{peodigital}, PMW 205 manages the Naval Enterprise Network (NEN). The office is responsible for the acquisition lifecycle of enterprise-wide networks, providing secure, seamless, and global computer connectivity for the Department of the Navy ashore.

The cornerstone ACAT I program managed by PMW 205 is the Next Generation Enterprise Network (NGEN) and its subsequent iteration, NGEN-Recompete (NGEN-R). NGEN serves as the successor to the Navy Marine Corps Intranet (NMCI), managing the massive logistical and technical burden of providing end-user hardware, software licensing, and enterprise business services to hundreds of thousands of users.

\textbf{Strategic Insights:} The magnitude of NGEN-R became acutely apparent during the global shift to remote work environments. PMW 205 demonstrated exceptional resource management by implementing agile contracting strategies during the NGEN-R transition. Leveraging supplemental funding via the Coronavirus Aid Relief and Securities (CARES) Act, PMW 205 accelerated the Navy Enterprise Office 365 migration and sustainment efforts, providing vastly expanded network access and productivity tools despite global hardware shortages and budget constraints. The seamless operation of this ACAT I network is a prerequisite for all shore-based administrative, logistical, and acquisition planning operations.

\subsubsection{PMW 790: Shore and Expeditionary Integration}
\label{ssubsec:pmw790}

\textbf{Program:} Deployable Joint Command and Control (DJC2)

\textbf{ACAT Status:} ACAT IAC

PMW 790 delivers resilient, interoperable shore and expeditionary C4I capabilities, ensuring that the infrastructure ashore matches the capacity of deployed fleets. The office's primary ACAT I capability is the Deployable Joint Command and Control (DJC2) system.

Originating as a Secretary of Defense priority transformation initiative, DJC2 provides a standardized, self-contained, rapidly deployable, and scalable joint command and control infrastructure to Geographic Combatant Commands (GCCs) and Combined/Joint Task Force (C/JTF) Commanders. By integrating computer-network-enabled C2 applications with self-powered tactical shelters, DJC2 allows commanders to establish a fully functional operations center in austere expeditionary environments rapidly. PMW 790 integrates this capability with over 60 unique shore and expeditionary networks, including the Shore Tactical Assured C2 (STACC) enterprise, to maintain global reach.

\subsubsection{PEO MLB: Manpower, Logistics and Business Solutions}
\label{ssubsec:peomlb}

\textbf{Program:} Navy Enterprise Resource Planning (Navy ERP)

\textbf{ACAT Status:} BCAT I

While strictly categorized as a Business Capability Acquisition Category (BCAT I) program, Navy ERP represents an enterprise IT investment equivalent in scale, complexity, and financial footprint to a traditional kinetic \ac{acat} I weapon system. Managed under \ac{peomlb} (formerly under PEO EIS/Sea Warrior program PMW 220), Navy ERP is the high-velocity back-office system that standardizes the Department of the Navy's business processes for acquisition, financial management, and logistics operations.

Combining commercial off-the-shelf software (primarily SAP architecture) with extensive business process reengineering, Navy ERP utilizes a single database to manage shared common data across functional communities. As of recent reporting, Navy ERP controls over 50 percent of the Navy's financial spending, manages global supply operations, and provides workforce management for over 65,000 personnel. By modernizing its code and upgrading software capabilities, Navy ERP serves as the fundamental enabler for the DoD's strategic goal of reforming business practices for greater performance and affordability, providing the auditability and financial tracking required for every other ACAT I program in the Navy's portfolio.
