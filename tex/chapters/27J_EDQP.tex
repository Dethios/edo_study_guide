\section{Engineering Duty Officer Qualification Program (EDQP)}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] The \ac{edqp} ensures each \ac{edo} completes technical education, masters core
  competencies, and demonstrates readiness for the 144X designator through a structured qualification
  process~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Governance.}] The \ac{edqp} hierarchy includes the designator advisor (Commander,
  \ac{navsea}), the \ac{edqb}, the \ac{edo} School, certifying and counseling officers, and each
  candidate~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Milestones.}] Five milestones drive completion: acceptance, postgraduate education, basic
  course graduation, qualification tour, and certification via oral examination~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Candidate duties.}] Candidates complete command-specific certification programs, process
  improvement requirements, required \ac{dau} courses, and the oral board within prescribed timelines~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Special qualifications.}] The ED Dolphin and \ac{iwo} qualifications are optional but
  structured programs with defined prerequisites and timelines for eligible \ac{edo}s~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{EDQP Objectives}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Professional education.}] Ensure each \ac{edo} completes a technical master's degree and earns
  an approved subspecialty code~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Core competencies.}] Build mastery in leadership, technical acumen, financial management,
  contracting, \ac{dod} acquisition, civilian personnel management, and \ac{bdar} fundamentals~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Mentorship and professionalism.}] Provide senior \ac{edo} mentorship, consistent community
  experience, and encourage acquisition workforce certification and professional membership~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{EDQP Hierarchy and Roles}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Designator advisor.}] The Commander, \ac{navsea} establishes \ac{edqp} requirements and acts
  as the technical review authority for training strategies~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Engineering Duty Qualification Board.}] The \ac{edqb} provides senior community oversight,
  recommends program changes, and supports curriculum review for the \ac{edo} School~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Certifying officer and counseling officer.}] The certifying officer (typically the candidate's
  \ac{co}) sets written guidance, while the counseling officer monitors progress and assists the certifying
  officer~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Candidate.}] Each candidate owns completion of the \ac{edqp} and is accountable for meeting
  milestone timelines~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{EDQP Milestones and Certification}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Milestone sequence.}] Acceptance, postgraduate education, basic course graduation,
  qualification tour (minimum one year), and certification via oral board are the required phases~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Qualification tour requirements.}] Candidates complete command-specific training,
  process-improvement requirements, an introduction to civilian supervision, \ac{dau} courses ACQ 0030
  and PMT 0141, and demonstrate \ac{edo} core competency fluency~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Oral board.}] The certification board is chaired by the certifying officer with at least three
  senior \ac{edo}s; candidates must demonstrate technical, organizational, and community knowledge and
  complete certification within two years of the basic course~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Candidate Responsibilities}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Core completion tasks.}] Candidates complete entrance requirements, technical master's
  degree, \ac{edo} basic course, command certification program, process improvement requirements, and
  required \ac{dau} courses~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Performance and timelines.}] Candidates perform satisfactorily during the qualification tour,
  complete the oral board within two years, and request extensions when circumstances warrant~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Feedback.}] Candidates provide lessons learned to improve the \ac{edo} School curriculum and
  \ac{edqp} process~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Lessons Learned}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Engaged leadership.}] Qualification success requires active certifying and counseling officer
  participation, frequent training touchpoints, and civilian subject matter expertise when available~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Common challenges.}] Candidates should plan early for docking observer requirements, select
  a process-improvement topic early enough to gather data, and schedule required courses in advance~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Board preparation.}] Oral boards reward clear communication, thorough study of the basic
  course, and the ability to reason through problems under pressure~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Extensions and waivers.}] Extension requests require timely coordination through the chain
  of command with adequate justification; do not wait until deadlines are imminent~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{ED Dolphin Program}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Program intent.}] The ED Dolphin program prepares \ac{edo}s without prior submarine
  qualification for submarine maintenance and acquisition roles and is voluntary~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Duration and scope.}] Completion typically takes 18 to 24 months and culminates in
  authorization to wear the ED Dolphin warfare insignia~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Key requirements.}] Requirements include submarine physicals, formal training, at least
  sixteen weeks of \\ac{ssbn}/\\ac{ssn} experience, an ED Dolphin journal, a submarine walkthrough
  examination, and an oral board of submarine-qualified officers~\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Information Warfare Officer Qualification}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Purpose.}] \\ac{iwo} qualification recognizes expertise in \\ac{iw} fields and is primarily
  intended for officers in information warfare communities~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\\textbf{Eligibility.}] \\ac{edo}s may qualify when assigned to \\ac{iw} billets, pre-register intent with
  \\ac{navifor}, and document qualifying \\ac{iw} experience and duties~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \item[\\textbf{Completion.}] Candidates complete \\ac{iwo} \\ac{pqs} and an oral qualification board within
  36 months, with \\ac{aqd} prerequisites met when required~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\\end{description}

\\subsection{Quick Review}
\\begin{itemize}
  \\item \\ac{edqp} aligns \\ac{edo} education, core competencies, and mentorship with qualification standards~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \\item Five milestones guide the path from acceptance to certification, including a minimum one-year qualification tour~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \\item Candidates must complete process improvement requirements, required \\ac{dau} courses, and the oral board on time~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \\item Lessons learned emphasize proactive scheduling, engaged leadership, and strong board preparation~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
  \\item ED Dolphin and \\ac{iwo} qualifications are optional programs with defined prerequisites and timelines~\\autocite{edo-5-2-2-edqp-2025}.
\\end{itemize}
