\section{Leading People}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Leadership core.}] Leading people is dynamic and shaped by personal experience, but effective leaders consistently model integrity, accountability, and a commitment to developing others~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Responsibility triad.}] Responsibility, accountability, and authority must be aligned for mission success and healthy command climates~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Intent-based leadership.}] Clear purpose, shared understanding, and competence enable decentralized initiative in complex environments~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Get Real, Get Better.}] The Navy-wide culture shift emphasizes transparency, problem solving, and learning teams to deliver warfighting advantage~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025,Navy-NLDF-GRGB-Leaders-Toolkit-2026}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Leadership Styles}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Vision, coaching, affiliative, democratic, pace-setting, and commanding styles may be situationally appropriate depending on mission and team needs~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Responsibility, Accountability, and Authority}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Responsibility.}] The moral, legal, or mental obligation to perform duties and own outcomes~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Accountability.}] The willingness to answer for actions and decisions~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Authority.}] The power to influence or command thought, opinion, or behavior~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Leadership Expectations}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{CNO 34 Day One Message.}] Focus on Foundry (industrial base and shipbuilding), Fleet (integrated force readiness), and Fight (lethality and survivability), with measures of success tied to on-time delivery, readiness, ordnance production, repair parts, and mastery-level training~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025,cno34-day-one-message-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Charge of Command.}] Lead with integrity, courage, and humility; invest in Sailors; own risk; prepare, stress plans, learn, and innovate; and honor the privilege and gravity of command~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025,cno-charge-of-command-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Get Real, Get Better}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Culture shift.}] Get Real, Get Better is the Navy standard for leadership and problem solving, building teams that self-assess, self-correct, and learn continuously~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025,Navy-NLDF-GRGB-Leaders-Toolkit-2026}.
  \item[\textbf{Get Real behaviors.}] Align on standards, act transparently, and embrace the red to expose performance gaps~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Get Better behaviors.}] Focus on what matters, use proven problem-solving methods, and fix or elevate barriers with urgency and accountability~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025,Navy-NLDF-GRGB-Leaders-Toolkit-2026}.
  \item[\textbf{Learning teams.}] Build trust, respect, and clear ownership while reinforcing collaboration and transparency~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Intent-Based Leadership}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Core idea.}] Intent-based leadership empowers people at all levels to make decisions aligned with mission intent rather than relying on centralized direction~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item[\textbf{Building blocks.}] Teams need shared understanding of purpose, clarity on each member's role, and technical competence to make good decisions~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Team of Teams}
\begin{description}
  \item[\textbf{Initiative culture.}] Historical examples (Nelson's "touch") show that winning complex fights requires a culture that rewards initiative and critical thinking beyond strict execution~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Where to Start}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Commit to continuous personal and professional development and expand leadership knowledge through reading, podcasts, and guided learning resources~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item Use Navy learning resources (e.g., DoD MWR Library, Libby App, and leadership networks) to build a sustainable learning habit~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Leadership relies on aligned responsibility, accountability, and authority~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
  \item Get Real, Get Better emphasizes transparency, problem solving, and learning teams~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025,Navy-NLDF-GRGB-Leaders-Toolkit-2026}.
  \item Intent-based leadership depends on shared purpose and competence~\autocite{edo-5-1-6-leading-people-2025}.
\end{itemize}
