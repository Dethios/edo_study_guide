\section{Availability Work Package Requirements and Development Stages}
An availability work package consolidates required work from maintenance records, authorized alterations, and \ac{tycom} readiness priorities, with \ac{csmp} screening as a primary input for candidate tasks.~\autocite{COMFLTFORCOMINST4790-3,edo-4-2-1-cno-availability-planning-2025} The package matures through staged planning products, moving from baseline identification to refined work definition and approval as execution approaches.~\autocite{COMFLTFORCOMINST4790-3,edo-4-2-1-cno-availability-planning-2025}

\section{Planning Milestones and Availability Type Differences}
Major availability planning uses milestone gates to stabilize scope and align funding, contracting, and scheduling, with earlier planning products supporting long-lead material ordering and schedule integration.~\autocite{COMFLTFORCOMINST4790-3,edo-4-2-1-cno-availability-planning-2025} \ac{cmav} planning emphasizes rolling execution windows, shorter lead times, and tighter scope control, while \ac{sra} planning uses more formalized milestones and broader work package integration because of larger scope and longer execution timelines.~\autocite{edo-4-2-1-cno-availability-planning-2025,edo-4-2-2-cno-availability-execution-2025}

\section{System Operational Verification Test Definition and Significance}
The system operational verification test is the structured verification that critical systems perform as intended following maintenance, enabling readiness certification and supporting safe return to operations.~\autocite{TS9090-310} \ac{sovt} results inform risk decisions, closeout timing, and any operational limitations that must be tracked after an availability.~\autocite{TS9090-310}

\section{Signed and Unsigned System Operational Verification Tests}
A signed \ac{sovt} indicates the required tests were completed, documented, and accepted by the responsible authority, reducing schedule uncertainty for closeout and post-availability certification.~\autocite{TS9090-310} An unsigned \ac{sovt} signals incomplete testing or unresolved discrepancies, which elevates schedule risk and may drive deferments, retesting, or extended restrictions until closure.~\autocite{TS9090-310}
