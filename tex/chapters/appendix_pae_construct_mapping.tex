\textbf{Current as of 2026-10-20; portfolio baseline: September coursebooks.}

\subsection{Summary}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \ac{pae} construct is now the public \ac{don} acquisition-reform baseline under \ac{asnrdanda}. It is not a 1:1 relabeling of \acp{syscom} or \acp{peo}; it is a portfolio-accountability model intended to align authority, responsibility, technical support, contracting support, sustainment, and industrial-base management around warfighting outcomes~\autocite{DON-PAE-Establishment-2026,DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026}.
  \item Public releases now identify nine \acp{pae}: Robotics and Autonomous Systems; Industrial Operations; Marine Corps; Maritime; Strategic Systems Programs; Undersea / DRPM Submarines; Aviation; Mission Systems; and Munitions~\autocite{DON-PAE-Establishment-2026,DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026}.
  \item The old ``source-limited'' caveat for PAE Mission Systems is no longer current. \ac{don} publicly established PAE Mission Systems on 11~May~2026 and named Mr.\ Jim Day as interim PAE~\autocite{DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026,DON-PAE-Mission-Systems-2026}.
  \item The user-provided PAE chart is a useful transition snapshot, but it predates the May~2026 addition of PAE Aviation, PAE Mission Systems, and PAE Munitions. Use the nine portfolios named in the September coursebook as the study baseline, with the later verified leadership and DRPM changes noted here.
  \item A board answer should not say \acp{syscom} disappeared. The public releases say PAEs will have direct authority over program offices and associated technical, contracting, and sustainment functions, and that about 70 percent of these associated functions and personnel will move from \acp{syscom} into PAEs. Technical authority, safety, certification, and engineering standards still need to be handled deliberately through the current technical authority chain until formal directives say otherwise~\autocite{DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{How to Draw the Current Board Chart}
\begin{description}[style=nextline, labelsep=0.5em, font=\bfseries]
  \item[Top.] \ac{secnav} at the top, with \ac{cno} and \ac{cmc} as Service leadership and requirements/readiness context.
  \item[Acquisition executive.] \ac{asnrdanda} under \ac{secnav}; current PTDO \ac{asnrdanda} is \ac{vadm} Seiko Okano as of 2026-10-20, following the September transition of William F.\ Mahan to Under Secretary duties. The May deputy roster is historical~\autocite{ExecutiveGov-ASNRDA-Sep2026,DONCIO-Okano-PTDO-2026}.
  \item[Portfolio layer.] The nine portfolios in the September coursebook form the acquisition baseline; directed DRPM reporting routes are exceptions to a generic ASN-only chain.
  \item[Support and authorities.] \acp{syscom}, warfare centers, shipyards, RMCs, NIWCs, contracting organizations, and sustainment organizations provide the technical, industrial, and execution depth being realigned into or in support of PAEs. Do not confuse portfolio accountability with operational command of forces.
\end{description}

\subsection{Portfolio Notes}

\subsubsection{PAE Aviation}
PAE Aviation was publicly established on 11~May~2026 and is led by VADM John Dougherty. Its public deputy lanes are Carrier Strike, Marine Corps Aviation, and Maritime ISR/NC3. PAE Aviation also oversees Mission Engineering, a Rapid Capability Cell linked to the \ac{don} Rapid Capabilities Office, and Disruptive Technology Development. The board-level reform cue is that PAEs are directed to prioritize commercial solutions, \ac{mosa}, government-purpose-rights interfaces, modular competition, supply-chain resiliency, and faster contracting mechanisms such as \acp{ota}~\autocite{NAVAIR-PAE-Aviation-2026}.

\subsubsection{PAE Industrial Operations}
PAE Industrial Operations was established in March~2026; Rear Adm.\ Todd Weeks relieved Downey as interim PAE on 6~August. The PAE provides unified leadership over four naval shipyards and seven Regional Maintenance Centers and is the Navy's accountable office for ship and submarine depot maintenance. This is a transfer of industrial and maintenance responsibilities from the legacy NAVSEA-centered model~\autocite{DVIDS-Weeks-PAEIO-Aug2026}.

\subsubsection{PAE Marine Corps}
PAE Marine Corps was formally established on 10~Apr~2026. It replaced PEO Land Systems and incorporated programs from Marine Corps Systems Command, while MCSC assumed an expanded role as the workforce provider for the Force Development Enterprise~\autocite{PAE-Marine-Corps-Establishment-2026}. Public Marine Corps PAE pages describe the PAE model as a single leader with cradle-to-grave responsibility for delivering defined mission capability, aligning technical, contracting, and sustainment personnel into the acquisition chain of command~\autocite{PAE-Marine-Corps-About-2026}. Public capability portfolios are C5ISRT, Protect, Sustain/Enable, Maneuver, and Fires~\autocite{PAE-Marine-Corps-Organization-2026}.

\subsubsection{PAE Maritime}
\ac{radm} Casey Moton is acting PAE Maritime in the official September~30 release; older Christopher Miller references predate his move to PAE / DRPM RAS~\autocite{PAE-Maritime-Moton-Sep2026,DON-DRPM-RAS-Aug2026}.

PAE Maritime delivers conventional surface ships, including Aircraft Carriers, Combatants, Expeditionary and Mine Warfare, Auxiliaries, and Modernization and Sustainment. The current lead is acting PAE Casey Moton; RDML Brian Metcalf is the combatants deputy on the September EDO roster. The May homepage's Miller/Moton leadership arrangement is superseded. SUPSHIPs align to this portfolio, and associated technical, contracting, and sustainment functions move with portfolio execution~\autocite{PAE-Maritime-Moton-Sep2026,edo-2-1-1-command-structures-2025,edo-2-1-2-navsea-organization-2025,DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026}.

\subsubsection{PAE Mission Systems}
PAE Mission Systems was publicly established on 11~May~2026 and Mr.\ Jim Day was named interim PAE~\autocite{DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026,DON-PAE-Mission-Systems-2026}. The public launch release says PAE Mission Systems pulls together mission-system elements from PEO C4I, PEO Digital, PEO IWS, PEO MLB, DRPM Overmatch, DRPM Long Range Naval Fires Office, DRPM Manpower IT Logistics ERP, the Minotaur program from NAVAIR's PMA-290 program office, NAVWAR, NAVSEA, NAVAIR, and MCSC~\autocite{DON-PAE-Mission-Systems-2026}. The board answer is: PAE Mission Systems is the central mission integrator for kill-chain closure, value-chain optimization, interoperability, and warfighting efficiency. Do not reduce it to NAVWAR; it spans information warfare, combat systems, digital/business systems, and cross-platform mission integration.

\subsubsection{PAE Munitions}
PAE Munitions was publicly established on 11~May~2026 and Mr.\ Paul Mann was named interim PAE~\autocite{DON-PAE-Expansion-May2026,DON-PAE-Munitions-2026}. Public pillars are Weapons Industrial Base, Air Weapons, Surface Weapons, and Advanced Capabilities and Innovation. The organization combines former PEO Unmanned Aviation and Strike Weapons offices PMA-201, PMA-208, PMA-242, PMA-259, PMA-280, and PMA-281 with former PEO IWS weapons offices IWS~3.0, IWS~11.0, and IWS~12.0. The public release also notes SM-3 is expected to transition from \ac{mda} in FY~2028 for production and sustainment~\autocite{DON-PAE-Munitions-2026}. The board answer is munitions industrial-base scale, weapons production capacity, and cross-platform weapons portfolio execution.

\subsubsection{PAE Robotics and Autonomous Systems}
PAE Robotics and Autonomous Systems was established in December~2025. On 5~August~2026 the Navy established DRPM RAS and named Christopher Miller acting DRPM and concurrently acting PAE RAS. The announcement specifies direct reporting to the Under Secretary's office for the DRPM role; do not force this route into a generic ASN-only PAE reporting rule. Gassler's April testimony is historical leadership evidence, not the current incumbent cue~\autocite{DON-DRPM-RAS-Aug2026,SASC-PAE-RAS-Gassler-2026}. The portfolio develops and fields unmanned/autonomous capabilities across the Navy and Marine Corps.

\subsubsection{PAE Strategic Systems Programs}
PAE Strategic Systems Programs is led by \ac{vadm} Douglas L.\ Williams, who relieved \ac{vadm} Johnny Wolfe Jr.\ on 12~June~2026. The handoff and Wolfe's retirement ceremony are complete. The portfolio covers strategic weapon sustainment, Columbia integration, D5LE2 modernization, SLCM-N, and Conventional Prompt Strike~\autocite{DVIDS-SSP-Handoff-Jun2026}.

\subsubsection{PAE Undersea / DRPM Submarines}
PAE Undersea / DRPM Submarines was announced on 16~Mar~2026 with VADM Robert Gaucher as interim PAE~\autocite{DON-PAE-Establishment-2026}. The May~2026 shipbuilding plan states that PAE Undersea is dual-hatted as the Direct Reporting Portfolio Manager for Submarines and will direct all activities to deliver nuclear-powered attack submarines, ballistic-missile submarines, and guided-missile submarines. The plan links the construct to the Deputy Secretary of War goal of reaching at least one Columbia SSBN and two Virginia SSNs per year by FY~2031~\autocite{DON-Shipbuilding-Plan-2026}. The board answer is submarine production, AUKUS/undersea enterprise alignment, and industrial-base execution.

\subsection{What Changed from Older Study Notes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Count changed from six baseline PAEs to nine public PAEs after 11~May~2026.
  \item PAE Mission Systems changed from source-limited/public-indicator status to a formal public \ac{don} announcement.
  \item PAE Aviation and PAE Munitions are no longer ``to be announced'' lanes.
  \item PAE Marine Corps formally sunsetted PEO Land Systems and moved responsibilities to PAE-MC.
  \item Current PTDO \ac{asnrdanda} is \ac{vadm} Seiko Okano as of 2026-10-20. Mahan moved to Under Secretary duties in September; the May~26 Mahan/Potter announcement is historical~\autocite{ExecutiveGov-ASNRDA-Sep2026,DONCIO-Okano-PTDO-2026}.
  \item The board distinction is no longer simply ``PEOs report to ASN(RD\&A) while SYSCOMs support.'' The current answer is ``PAEs are portfolio-accountable officials under ASN(RD\&A); legacy PEO/SYSCOM lanes still matter for program recall, technical authority, workforce, contracting, and sustainment execution during transition.''
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Board Bottom Line}
\begin{quote}
The PAE restructuring moves Navy acquisition from a program-by-program, SYSCOM/PEO matrix toward accountable capability portfolios. PAEs own portfolio outcomes, industrial-base risk, and associated technical, contracting, and sustainment functions. NAVSEA and warfare centers provide engineering and execution depth within current charters and delegated technical authorities.
\end{quote}

\subsection{Quick Review}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Current board count is nine \acp{pae}: Aviation, Industrial Operations, Marine Corps, Maritime, Mission Systems, Munitions, Robotics and Autonomous Systems, Strategic Systems Programs, and Undersea / DRPM Submarines.
  \item Do not describe \acp{pae} as a simple rename of \acp{peo}; they are portfolio-accountable officials under \ac{asnrdanda}.
  \item Do not say \acp{syscom} disappeared; they still matter for technical authority, workforce depth, contracting/sustainment support, and transition execution.
  \item PAE Mission Systems is cross-enterprise mission integration, not just NAVWAR.
  \item Verify leader names before a name-only answer because several \ac{pae} and flag billets are active transition cues.
\end{itemize}
