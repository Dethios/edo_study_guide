Warfighter Acquisition System: Replacing JCIDS in Requirements and Acquisition
In 2025, the Department of Defense undertook sweeping reform to accelerate capability development by replacing the Joint Capabilities Integration and Development System (JCIDS) with a streamlined, warfighter-centric framework. This shift emphasizes solving operational problems quickly and efficiently, leveraging experimentation, direct service-level authority, and agile acquisition practices.
Governing Policies and Official Guidance
Executive Order 14265 and DoD-level memoranda issued in 2025 ordered the JCIDS sunset and the transition to a new framework.
JROC Role Reduction: The Joint Requirements Oversight Council (JROC) now focuses only on a short list of joint “Key Operational Problems” (KOPs).
Requirements and Resourcing Alignment Board (RRAB): New high-level board co-chaired by the Deputy Secretary of Defense and the Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs to align problem-solving with funding.
Joint Acceleration Reserve (JAR): New rapid-reaction fund to bridge the “valley of death” in prototyping.
Mission Engineering and Integration Activity (MEIA): Cross-service experiments to define needs through demonstration and prototyping.
Portfolio Acquisition Executives (PAEs): Service-led portfolios with direct accountability and authority for tailoring and delivery.

Warfighter Role
Warfighter input filtered via layers

Innovation Access
Bureaucratic barrier to non-traditionals
Startups, COTS, non-trads welcomed via early experimentation and commercial pathways
Implications for EDO and Acquisition Professionals
Expect fewer staff products and more warfighter interaction.
Field-driven prototyping replaces requirements writing.
Faster authority pathways and less HQ review.
Portfolio ownership and time-based metrics are becoming standard.
Training and DAU courses will focus on agility, not compliance.
